Scenario ID: SCN-021
Title: Exclude a topic — narrow by carving one out
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Beyond stacking topics together (include/AND, SCN-008), the client can carve a
topic OUT: "recipes but not baking", or "my to-read pile minus anything work".
Each topic in the Topics bar has two explicit controls: click the label to
INCLUDE it (blue), and click a small "−" on it to EXCLUDE it (red). This
explicit two-control design is chosen deliberately over a clever single-click
tri-state cycle, because exclusion is easy to trigger by accident and must never
be a hidden "second click means the opposite".

An excluded topic is shown red and struck-through, and a plain-language summary
reads "showing recipes but not baking", so an exclusion can NEVER work in secret
— the client always sees exactly what is being left out and can switch it back
off. Filtering: a link is shown if it has all included topics and none of the
excluded ones. Exclusions are part of "where I was standing" and are remembered
by a saved view (SCN-020).

## Gherkin
Feature: Exclude a topic

  Scenario: Narrow by excluding
    Given links labelled "recipes", some also "baking"
    When the client includes "recipes" and excludes "baking"
    Then only recipe links that are not baking are shown
    And "baking" is shown red and struck-through
    And a plain-language line states what is excluded

  Scenario: Exclude within a lens
    Given the To-read pile
    When the client excludes "work"
    Then unread links not labelled work are shown

  Scenario: A saved view remembers exclusions
    When the client saves a view that excludes a topic
    Then recalling it restores the exclusion

## Assumptions
- Explicit include + "−" exclude, not a tri-state click. (client confirmed)
- Excluded topics visibly red/struck + plain-language summary; never silent.
  (client confirmed)
- Exclusions are part of the saved-view bundle. (client confirmed)
