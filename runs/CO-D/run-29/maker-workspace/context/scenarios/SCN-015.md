Scenario ID: SCN-015
Title: Retain saved bookmark data when the original page later disappears
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-015
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
If a webpage becomes unavailable after it was saved, the app retains the saved title, description, tags, personal notes, site icon, preview, and address unchanged. Selecting the title may lead to the unavailable external page, but the client's saved record remains valuable and is neither deleted nor quietly replaced.

## Gherkin
Feature: Preserve bookmark records over time

  Scenario: Keep a bookmark after its original webpage disappears
    Given a bookmark was saved while its original webpage was available
    And the bookmark has a title, description, tags, personal notes, site icon, preview, and address
    When the original webpage later becomes unavailable
    Then every saved bookmark detail remains unchanged
    And the bookmark remains in the collection
    And selecting its title still uses the saved address

## Assumptions
- The app does not delete or replace saved data in response to later external page availability.
- Keeping a readable archived copy of the original page is explicitly deferred to a later cycle.
