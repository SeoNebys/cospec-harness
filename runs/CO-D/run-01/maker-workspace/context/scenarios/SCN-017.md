Scenario ID: SCN-017
Title: Preserve reviewed bookmark details over time
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Once bookmark details have been reviewed and saved, they belong to the person and remain a stable snapshot. Later changes to the original page never silently replace the saved title, description, site icon, or picture, especially wording the person edited. Fetching newer page details can occur only through a deliberate action explored separately.

## Gherkin
Feature: Stable saved bookmark details

  Scenario: Original page details change after saving
    Given a bookmark has reviewed and saved details
    And the original page later changes its title, description, icon, or picture
    When the person returns to the bookmark collection
    Then the saved bookmark still shows the details as the person left them
    And no saved detail is automatically replaced

## Assumptions
- Automatically gathered details become a saved snapshot once approved.
- Personal edits receive the same protection as other reviewed details.
