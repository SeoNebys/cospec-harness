Scenario ID: SCN-021
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark after confirmation
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can permanently delete a bookmark together with its notes and label assignments. Because the action cannot be undone, a confirmation names what will be removed and requires an explicit Delete permanently action before the bookmark disappears from the collection.

## Gherkin
Feature: Removing bookmarks

  Scenario: Confirm permanent bookmark deletion
    Given a saved bookmark may contain personal notes and labels
    When the person chooses to delete the bookmark
    Then a confirmation warns that the bookmark and its personal information will be removed
    When the person confirms "Delete permanently"
    Then the bookmark, its notes, and its label assignments are removed from the collection

  Scenario: Cancel permanent bookmark deletion
    Given the permanent-deletion confirmation is open
    When the person chooses to keep the bookmark
    Then the confirmation closes
    And the bookmark and all of its information remain unchanged

## Assumptions
- Permanent deletion cannot be undone after confirmation.
- The confirmation is shown before any bookmark data is removed.
