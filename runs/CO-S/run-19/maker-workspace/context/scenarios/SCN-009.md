Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-009
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

The client can choose Delete permanently from a bookmark's secondary-actions menu. Before anything is removed, a warning dialog names the bookmark, states that deletion cannot be undone, and requires deliberate confirmation. Confirming permanently removes the bookmark and all of its saved details. It does not move the bookmark to Archive and offers no undo or recovery.

## Gherkin

Feature: Permanently delete bookmarks

  Scenario: Confirm permanent deletion
    Given a bookmark is visible in All bookmarks
    When the user opens the bookmark's secondary-actions menu
    And chooses "Delete permanently"
    Then a warning dialog names the bookmark
    And the warning states that deletion cannot be undone
    And the bookmark remains present until the user confirms
    When the user confirms "Delete permanently"
    Then the bookmark and all its saved details are removed
    And the bookmark is not present in All bookmarks
    And the bookmark is not present in Archive
    And no undo or recovery action is offered

## Assumptions

- Permanent deletion is placed in the secondary-actions menu.
- The user must confirm in a warning dialog rather than through a second inline click.
- Deletion removes all stored information belonging to the bookmark.
