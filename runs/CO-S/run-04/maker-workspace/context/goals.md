# Business goal

## Cycle 1 — confirmed 2026-07-14

### Goal statement (client's language)

One trusted spot to drop a link and actually get back to it later — instead of
links scattered across the browser, texts-to-self, and random notes, where they
"just disappear into a black hole."

### What is core

- Bringing links together into **one place** (a big part of the win).
- **Finding a link again** and getting back to it later (the point of the app).

### How the client expects to retrieve

Both, depending on the moment:
- Sometimes roughly remembers what it was → wants to **search** for it.
- Other times → wants to **scroll/browse** through everything saved and spot it.

### Client utterances (verbatim, do not reinterpret)

- "I save links all over the place — some in my browser, some I text to myself,
  a few in random notes — and then when I actually want one again I can never
  find it. Feels like they just disappear into a black hole. I want one spot
  where I can drop a link and actually get back to it later."
- "Having them in one place is a big part of it, but yeah — being able to
  actually find one again is the point."
- "Sometimes I kind of remember what it was and want to search for it, other
  times I just want to scroll through what I've saved and spot it. Both would
  be handy."
