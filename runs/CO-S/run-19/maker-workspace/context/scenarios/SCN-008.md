Scenario ID: SCN-008
Title: Archive and restore a bookmark
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-008
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

The client can deliberately choose Archive from a bookmark's secondary-actions menu. The bookmark is removed from All bookmarks without its details being changed or deleted and remains available in the Archive section. Choosing Restore to library returns it to the everyday list. Archive is visually and behaviorally distinct from permanent deletion.

## Gherkin

Feature: Archive bookmarks

  Scenario: Tuck a bookmark away without deleting it
    Given a bookmark is visible in All bookmarks
    When the user opens the bookmark's secondary-actions menu
    And chooses "Archive"
    Then the bookmark is removed from All bookmarks
    And the bookmark appears in Archive with its details unchanged

  Scenario: Restore an archived bookmark
    Given a bookmark is visible in Archive
    When the user chooses "Restore to library"
    Then the bookmark is removed from Archive
    And the bookmark reappears in All bookmarks with its details unchanged

## Assumptions

- Archive is placed in the secondary-actions menu rather than directly on the card.
- Archiving is reversible and does not delete bookmark data.
- Restoring returns the bookmark to the everyday list.
