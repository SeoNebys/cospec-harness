Scenario ID: SCN-014
Title: Remove a bookmark from read later without deleting it
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-014
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Clicking a filled star in the Read later list removes the bookmark from that list and clearly confirms the removal. The saved bookmark remains in the person's overall collection; removing it from Read later does not delete it.

## Gherkin
Feature: Removing bookmarks from read later

  Scenario: Unmark a bookmark from the Read later list
    Given a saved bookmark is marked for later and appears in Read later
    When the person clicks its filled star
    Then the bookmark is removed from the Read later list
    And the app confirms its removal from that list
    And the bookmark remains saved in All bookmarks

## Assumptions
- The same star control both adds and removes the read-later marker.
- Removing a read-later marker never deletes the bookmark.
