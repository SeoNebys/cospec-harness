Opening vs. renaming — two ways

Same links in both. A normal click always opens the page. The panels differ only in how you start a rename.

Option A — Hover pencil

Click a name to open it. Hover over a link and a small pencil (✎) appears — click the pencil to rename in place.

    Option B — Double-click to rename

    Click a name once to open it. Double-click the name to rename it in place.
