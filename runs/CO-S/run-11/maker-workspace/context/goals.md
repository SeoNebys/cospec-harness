# Business goal statement

## Cycle 1 — Session 1 (2026-09-19)

### The problem (client's words)
> "My browser bookmarks have become a pile of links I rarely find again, so the
> main goal is quick retrieval later."

### The goal
A personal web app for saving and managing bookmarks, where the single most
important outcome is **finding a saved link again quickly later**.

### What the client wants (client's words, lightly organised)
- "save a link quickly, have its basic details filled in automatically"
- "organize it with tags"
- "Search is more important to me than keeping everything in perfect folders."
- "a simple way to separate things I still intend to read from things I've
  finished with, without having to delete them"

### Emphasis
- Quick retrieval / search is **the** heart of the app (client confirmed).
- Auto-filled details, tags, and to-read/finished status are means that serve
  retrieval.

### Scope
- Personal use only. No team sharing (client confirmed).

### Interface form
- Web app opened in a browser (client confirmed).
