Scenario ID: SCN-021
Title: Cancel bookmark editing or deletion without changing anything
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-021
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Cancelling an edit discards every unsaved change and leaves the bookmark card and stored details exactly as they were. Cancelling from the permanent-delete confirmation removes nothing: the bookmark, title, notes, tags, Read later membership, and saved copy all remain untouched.

## Gherkin
Feature: Cancel bookmark management actions safely

  Scenario: Cancel unsaved bookmark edits
    Given a saved bookmark is open for editing
    And the client has changed its title without saving
    When the client cancels editing
    Then the editor closes
    And the unsaved title change is discarded
    And every stored bookmark detail remains unchanged

  Scenario: Cancel permanent deletion
    Given the permanent-delete confirmation is open for a bookmark
    When the client cancels deletion
    Then the confirmation closes
    And the bookmark remains in the library
    And its title, notes, tags, Read later membership, and saved copy remain unchanged

## Assumptions
- Cancel means discard, with no draft retained.
- No deletion side effect occurs before explicit permanent confirmation.
