Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: Sign in so bookmarks follow the user across devices
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The app is for a single owner. The first time, the owner creates an account with
an email and a password (with password confirmation). After that, opening the app
on any device where they are not signed in shows a sign-in screen; entering the
correct email and password signs them in and loads their bookmarks. Because the
bookmarks are kept centrally against the account, the same links appear on every
device the owner signs in from. A "Keep me signed in on this device" option is
ticked by default, so on their own devices they are not asked for the password on
every visit; they sign in again only after signing out or on a new device. The
client approved email+password identification, keep-signed-in by default, and a
one-time account setup followed by ordinary sign-in.

## Gherkin
Feature: Account and cross-device access

  Scenario: Create the account the first time
    Given no account exists yet
    When the owner enters an email, a password, and a matching confirmation
    And chooses to create the account
    Then the account is created and the owner reaches their (empty) bookmarks

  Scenario: Sign in on a new device loads the same bookmarks
    Given the owner has saved bookmarks under their account
    And they open the app on a device where they are not signed in
    When they enter their correct email and password
    Then they are signed in
    And their existing bookmarks are shown, identical to their other devices

  Scenario: Stay signed in on a trusted device
    Given the owner signed in with "Keep me signed in" ticked
    When they later reopen the app on the same device
    Then they are still signed in without re-entering the password

  Scenario: Wrong credentials are rejected
    Given the sign-in screen is shown
    When the owner enters an email/password that do not match the account
    Then sign-in is refused and they remain on the sign-in screen

## Assumptions
- Single-owner app: exactly one account; no separate collections for other people.
- Bookmarks are stored centrally per account so they are shared across devices.
- Identification is by email + password; a password confirmation is required at
  account creation.
- "Keep me signed in" persists the session on that device until sign-out.
- A sign-out action exists to end the session on a device (returns to sign-in).
- The specific mechanism/strength of password storage and sessions is a
  non-functional concern (see non-functional-backlog.md), not fixed here.
