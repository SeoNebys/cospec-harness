Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark, with confirmation (distinct from Archive)
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

Every bookmark card (in the Collection, Reading list, and Archive) offers a
Delete action, distinct from Archive. Delete does not remove immediately: it asks
for explicit confirmation with a clear warning that the removal is permanent and
cannot be undone, offering "Delete permanently" and "Cancel". Cancel leaves
everything untouched. Confirming removes the bookmark for good. Archiving remains
a separate, reversible action; the client does not have to archive a bookmark
before deleting it.

## Gherkin

Feature: Deleting a bookmark

  Scenario: Delete asks for confirmation
    Given any bookmark
    When I choose Delete
    Then I am warned that the removal is permanent and cannot be undone
    And I am offered "Delete permanently" and "Cancel"

  Scenario: Cancel leaves the bookmark untouched
    Given the delete confirmation is showing
    When I choose Cancel
    Then the bookmark remains unchanged

  Scenario: Confirm removes the bookmark for good
    Given the delete confirmation is showing
    When I choose "Delete permanently"
    Then the bookmark is removed and no longer appears in any view

  Scenario: Delete is available without archiving first
    Given an active (non-archived) bookmark
    Then I can delete it directly, without archiving it first

## Assumptions
- Permanent deletion is irreversible in this cycle (no separate trash/undo beyond
  the confirmation step).
