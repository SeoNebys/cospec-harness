## Approved behaviour
Working through the pile has two rhythms. One at a time: a single "✓ Read"
button on a card knocks it off instantly (the "one down" hit), sliding it out as
the count drops. In a batch: ticking checkboxes reveals a bottom action bar that
appears only once something is selected; the client can "Mark as read" several
together (the "right, sorted" tidy feeling), with a "Select all" for wiping the
whole pile after a good reading week.

There are two honest ways to leave the pile:
- "✓ Read" — the client read it; it becomes "dealt with" and earns the badge.
- "Not going to read" — the client gives up on it; it rejoins the library
  unflagged with NO "read" badge, so the badge never becomes a lie.
Both are available singly and in bulk.

## Gherkin
Feature: Drain the read-later pile

  Scenario: Knock off one at a time
    Given the To-read pile has links
    When the client clicks "✓ Read" on one
    Then it slides out of the pile and the count drops
    And it is recorded as dealt-with (read)

  Scenario: Sweep several at once
    When the client ticks several links
    Then a batch action bar appears
    When the client chooses "Mark as read"
    Then all selected links leave the pile together as read
    And "Select all" selects the whole current pile

  Scenario: Two honest exits
    When the client chooses "Not going to read" on a link (singly or in bulk)
    Then it leaves the pile without a read badge
    And rejoins the library unflagged
    And this is distinct from links marked read

## Assumptions
- Batch bar appears only after selection, never cluttering single-item flow.
  (client confirmed)
- "Read" earns the badge; "not going to read" does not. (client confirmed)
