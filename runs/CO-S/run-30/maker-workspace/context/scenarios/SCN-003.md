Scenario ID: SCN-003
Title: Open a saved bookmark without losing the library
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The whole bookmark card acts as a link. Clicking it opens the saved page in a new browser tab while the bookmark library remains open in its original tab and retains the client's place.

## Gherkin
Feature: Opening bookmarks

  Scenario: Open the saved page from its bookmark card
    Given the library contains a bookmark for a page
    When the client clicks the bookmark card
    Then the saved page opens in a new browser tab
    And the bookmark library remains open in the original tab

## Assumptions
- A bookmark card has one saved destination.
- The whole non-control surface of the card is the link to that destination.
- The browser permits the client-initiated new tab to open.
