Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Cancel permanent deletion without changing the bookmark
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Choosing Keep bookmark in the permanent-deletion warning closes the warning and leaves the named bookmark completely unchanged in its current destination.

## Gherkin
Feature: Back out of permanent deletion safely

  Scenario: Cancel the deletion warning
    Given the permanent-deletion warning is open for a bookmark
    When the client chooses Keep bookmark
    Then the warning closes
    And the bookmark remains unchanged in its current destination

## Assumptions
- Opening the warning itself never mutates bookmark data.
