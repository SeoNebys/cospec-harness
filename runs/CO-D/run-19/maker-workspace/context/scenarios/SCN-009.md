Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark with explicit confirmation
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Permanent deletion is available from any bookmark card's extra-actions menu and does not require first moving the bookmark through Archive. It is visually separated from Move to Archive and marked as the serious action. Selecting it opens a warning that names the bookmark, states that deletion cannot be undone or restored from Archive, and requires deliberate confirmation. Only confirming permanently removes the bookmark from everywhere.

## Gherkin
Feature: Permanently remove an unwanted bookmark

  Scenario: Delete a bookmark from any active card
    Given a bookmark card is shown anywhere outside or inside Archive
    When the client opens its extra-actions menu
    And chooses Delete permanently
    Then a warning names the bookmark
    And the warning says deletion removes it from everywhere and cannot be undone
    And the bookmark remains present until the client confirms
    When the client confirms Delete permanently
    Then the bookmark is removed from every library destination
    And the client is told it was deleted permanently

## Assumptions
- The explicit named confirmation is the safety mechanism; archiving first is not required.
- Permanent deletion also removes the bookmark's Read Later membership and its label associations.
- Deletion does not remove reusable labels from the wider label library.
