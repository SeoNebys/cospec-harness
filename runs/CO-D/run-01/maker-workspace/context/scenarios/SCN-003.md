Scenario ID: SCN-003
Title: Rename a saved link in place
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client can quickly change a saved link's title without leaving the list.
Pressing Rename on the card turns the title into an editable field; the client
types their own words and saves, and the card shows the new title. This is for
fast title fixes; a fuller "edit everything" space (note, summary, labels,
address) is planned separately (see goals parking lot).

## Gherkin
Feature: Rename a saved link

  Scenario: Renaming a link in place
    Given the client has a saved link
    When the client presses Rename, edits the title, and saves
    Then the card shows the client's chosen title
    And no page navigation occurs

  Scenario: Starting a rename does not open the link
    Given the client has a saved link
    When the client presses Rename on that link
    Then the link's page does not open
    And the title becomes editable in place

## Assumptions
- Renaming replaces the displayed title with the client's words; the auto title
  is overridden by the client's choice.
- An empty title is not saved (the previous title is kept).
- Quick rename coexists with a future full edit view (not instead of it).
