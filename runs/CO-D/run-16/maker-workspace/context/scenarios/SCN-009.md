Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Separate "to read" from "finished" without deleting
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Each bookmark has a reading status: "To read" or "Finished". Three tabs — All,
To read, and Finished — let the client view bookmarks by status, each tab
showing a count. On a bookmark, a button toggles its status: "Mark as finished"
moves it to Finished, and "Move back to To read" returns it. Finishing a
bookmark never deletes it or loses any detail; finished bookmarks remain in All
and stay fully searchable. Search operates within the currently selected tab.

## Gherkin
Feature: Reading status

  Scenario: Mark a bookmark finished
    Given a bookmark with status "To read"
    When the client marks it as finished
    Then its status becomes "Finished"
    And it is not deleted and keeps all its details
    And it no longer appears under the "To read" tab but remains under "All"

  Scenario: Move a finished bookmark back to to-read
    Given a bookmark with status "Finished"
    When the client moves it back to "To read"
    Then its status becomes "To read" and it reappears under "To read"

  Scenario: View by status with counts
    Given bookmarks of both statuses exist
    When the client selects the All, To read, or Finished tab
    Then only the bookmarks matching that tab are shown, with a count
    And search filters within the selected tab

  Scenario: Empty status views
    Given no bookmarks match the selected status tab
    Then an appropriate empty-state message for that tab is shown

## Assumptions
- Finished bookmarks stay in "All" and remain searchable.
