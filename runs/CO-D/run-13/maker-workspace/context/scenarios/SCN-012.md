Scenario ID: SCN-012
Title: Saved-time display — relative when recent, dated after about a week
Type: Edge Case (temporal)
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

Each entry shows when it was saved:
- Less than about a week ago: a relative label ("just now", "12 min ago",
  "3 h ago", "2 d ago").
- About a week or older: an actual date (e.g. "23 Sep"), including the year when
  the year is not the current year (e.g. "14 Aug 2024").

The client approved relative-for-recent and dated-after-about-a-week with the
year shown when relevant.

## Gherkin

Feature: Saved-time display

  Scenario: Recent save shows relative time
    Given a link was saved less than about a week ago
    Then its time is shown relatively (e.g. "3 h ago")

  Scenario: Older save shows a date
    Given a link was saved about a week ago or more, in the current year
    Then its time is shown as a day and month (e.g. "23 Sep")

  Scenario: Older save from a previous year shows the year
    Given a link was saved in a previous year
    Then its date includes the year (e.g. "14 Aug 2024")

## Assumptions
- "About a week" ≈ 7 days (tunable).
