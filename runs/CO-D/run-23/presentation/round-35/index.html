Ideas & practice

Building a resilient personal knowledge base

By Mara Chen · Originally from fieldnotes.example/articles/resilient-knowledge

A useful personal library is more than a collection of links. It preserves the context that made an idea worth saving in the first place.

Start by capturing enough information to recognize each source later. A clear title, a short summary, and your own notes turn an address into something meaningful and retrievable.

Over time, websites move and pages change. Keeping a readable copy means the knowledge you deliberately collected does not disappear along with its original location.
