Scenario ID: SCN-018
Title: Save and reopen a frequently used search
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-018
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can save the current guided search directly from its results and give it a recognizable name. The named search appears under Saved searches in the sidebar. Selecting it once restores the same combined conditions and live results. The saved search stores conditions rather than a frozen list, so bookmarks saved later are included whenever they match.

## Gherkin
Feature: Saved searches

  Scenario: Save and reopen a combined search
    Given a guided search has multiple active conditions
    And its current results are visible
    When the person chooses Save this search
    And names it "Accessible design"
    Then "Accessible design" appears under Saved searches in the sidebar
    When the person selects "Accessible design"
    Then the saved conditions are restored
    And their current matching bookmarks are shown

  Scenario: Include future matching bookmarks
    Given a saved search stores a set of conditions
    And a bookmark saved later satisfies those conditions
    When the person opens the saved search
    Then the later bookmark is included in the results

## Assumptions
- Saved searches appear in the sidebar.
- Saving a search stores its conditions, not its current result identities.
- Opening a saved search requires one click.
