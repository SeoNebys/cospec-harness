# Business goal statement

## Cycle 1 — Bookmark saving and management app

### The client's request (verbatim)

> I want to build an app to save and manage bookmarks.

> Right now my bookmarks are a growing pile in the browser, and I often can't
> remember where I saved something or why it mattered. I want one personal place
> where I can quickly save a link, have the useful page details filled in for me,
> add my own tags or notes, and reliably find it again later.
>
> I'd also like to separate things I still intend to read from things I've
> finished with, without having to delete useful links. The real success for me
> is that saving stays effortless, but the collection remains useful instead of
> becoming another forgotten pile.

> Yes, that captures it. The heart of it is being able to trust that I can save
> something quickly now and still find and understand why I saved it months
> later. I don't want organizing each link to feel like work, but I do want
> enough structure that the collection stays useful as it grows.

### Confirmed goal

The heart of the goal is **trust that a link saved quickly now can still be
found and understood months later**. Saving must feel effortless (not "work"),
while the collection retains **enough structure to stay useful as it grows**,
rather than decaying into a second forgotten pile.

### Means named by the client (to be explored as scenarios, not assumed)

- Quick, effortless saving of a link
- Useful page details filled in automatically
- The client's own tags or notes
- Reliable finding later
- Separating "still intend to read" from "finished with" without deleting

### Status

- Goal confirmed by client: yes
- Interface form: **Web app**, opened in a browser. Primarily used on a
  computer, but should remain comfortable to use from a phone (see
  non-functional backlog).
