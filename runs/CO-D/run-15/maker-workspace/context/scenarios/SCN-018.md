Scenario ID: SCN-018
Title: Reject a failed sign-in without revealing account details
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When the email-and-password combination is wrong, the client remains signed out. The app displays one general mismatch message that does not reveal which field failed, retains the entered email, clears the failed password, and focuses the password field for another attempt.

## Gherkin

Feature: Access a private bookmark collection

  Scenario: Attempt to sign in with a wrong password
    Given the client is signed out
    And the client has entered the account email and a wrong password
    When the client attempts to sign in
    Then the client remains signed out
    And a general mismatch message is shown
    And the message does not reveal whether the email or password failed
    And the email remains entered
    And the password is cleared
    And the password field is ready for another attempt

## Assumptions

- Failed sign-in feedback must not confirm whether a particular account email exists.
