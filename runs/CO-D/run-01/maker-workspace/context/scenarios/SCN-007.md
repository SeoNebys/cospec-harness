Scenario ID: SCN-007
Title: Browse links by label
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A "Labels" bar near the top lists all the client's labels so they can glance at
their topics and jump to one without hunting. Clicking a label — either in the
Labels bar or on a card's chip — narrows the list to the links carrying that
label. Clicking the active label again clears the filter. Filtering is by one
label at a time.

## Gherkin
Feature: Browse by label

  Scenario: Filter to a label from the Labels bar
    Given the client has links with various labels
    When the client clicks a label in the Labels bar
    Then only links carrying that label are shown
    And clicking the same label again restores the full list

  Scenario: Filter to a label from a card chip
    Given a link shows its label chips
    When the client clicks a label chip on the card
    Then the list narrows to that label
    And the link's page does not open

## Assumptions
- One label filter is active at a time (combining labels is parked with the
  pointed/boolean-search family — see goals parking lot).
- The Labels bar reflects all labels currently in use across saved links.
