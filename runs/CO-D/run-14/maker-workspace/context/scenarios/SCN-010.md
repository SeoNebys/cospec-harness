Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Read a saved copy when the original page disappears
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-010
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When a bookmark is first saved, the site quietly captures a readable copy without requiring a separate action. If the original page later cannot be reached, the client receives a clear notice and can open the saved copy. The copy preserves the article text, inline images, author, original publication details, source site, and capture date. It need not reproduce the original site's interactive behavior.

## Gherkin
Feature: Preserve bookmarked page content

  Scenario: Capture a readable copy automatically
    Given the client saves a reachable page as a bookmark
    When the bookmark is created
    Then a readable copy is captured without another client action
    And the copy retains the readable text, inline images, author, publication details, and source information

  Scenario: Open the saved copy after the original disappears
    Given a bookmark has a saved copy
    And its original page is no longer reachable
    When the client tries to open the original
    Then the site clearly explains that the original is unavailable
    And offers the saved copy
    When the client opens the saved copy
    Then its preserved readable content and provenance are displayed

## Assumptions
- Saved-copy capture happens automatically during the initial save.
- The saved copy prioritizes readable content and inline images, not scripts or interactive site features.
- Provenance includes the author and publication details when the original page provided them, plus the original source and capture date.
