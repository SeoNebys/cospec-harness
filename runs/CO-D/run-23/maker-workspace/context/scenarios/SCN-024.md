Scenario ID: SCN-024
Title: Keep unusually long bookmark cards compact and expandable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-024
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
An unusually long page title and description do not make one bookmark card dominate the everyday list. The card keeps a tidy, predictable compact size by showing a limited amount of each field. A Show full details action expands that card to reveal the complete title and description, and Show less returns it to the compact form.

## Gherkin
Feature: Display bookmarks with unusually long details

  Scenario: Scan a compact card with long text
    Given a bookmark has an unusually long title and description
    When it appears in the everyday list
    Then its title and description are limited to a compact predictable card size
    And the surrounding bookmarks are not pushed far down the list
    And a Show full details action is available

  Scenario: Expand and collapse long details
    Given a compact bookmark card hides part of its long title and description
    When the person chooses Show full details
    Then the complete title and description are visible
    When the person chooses Show less
    Then the card returns to its compact size

## Assumptions
- Compacting long details affects display only and does not truncate stored content.
