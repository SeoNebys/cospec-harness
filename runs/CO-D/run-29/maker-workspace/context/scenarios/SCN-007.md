Scenario ID: SCN-007
Title: Search by tags, combinations, exclusions, and exact phrases
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-007
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A `#` prefix searches for a complete tag without regard to letter case. The client can explicitly combine search words, tag searches, and groups with `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, and parentheses. `NOT` excludes matching bookmarks. Text inside quotes is matched as one exact phrase in that order, and words such as “and” inside quotes are treated as searchable text rather than instructions. A compact search such as `Rome #article #book` remains a shortcut for bookmarks matching “Rome” and either listed tag; when explicit operators are present, they control the combination.

## Gherkin
Feature: Expressive bookmark search

  Scenario: Search for a complete tag
    Given bookmarks have different tags
    When the client searches for a tag using a # prefix with any letter case
    Then bookmarks carrying that complete tag are shown
    And bookmarks without that tag are hidden

  Scenario: Combine and group search conditions explicitly
    Given the collection contains a Rome article and a Rome book
    When the client searches for `Rome AND (#article OR #book)`
    Then the Rome article is shown
    And the Rome book is shown
    And bookmarks that do not satisfy the grouped combination are hidden

  Scenario: Exclude bookmarks with NOT
    Given the collection contains Rome bookmarks with different tags
    When the client searches for `Rome AND NOT #book`
    Then Rome bookmarks not tagged book are shown
    And Rome bookmarks tagged book are hidden

  Scenario: Search an exact phrase containing an operator word
    Given a bookmark contains the phrase `rock and roll`
    When the client searches for `"rock and roll"`
    Then that bookmark is shown
    And `and` is matched as ordinary text
    And the phrase must occur together in that order

  Scenario: Use the compact multiple-tag shortcut
    Given bookmarks contain the word Rome and have different tags
    When the client searches for `Rome #article #book` without explicit operators
    Then bookmarks containing Rome and tagged article or book are shown

## Assumptions
- Operator words are recognised without regard to letter case when unquoted.
- Explicit NOT is evaluated before AND, and AND before OR, unless parentheses group the conditions differently.
- Ordinary search matching within explicit expressions remains case-insensitive.
