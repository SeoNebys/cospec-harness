# Business goal statement

## Cycle 1 — recorded SESSION-001

### Client's goal (in the client's own words)

> Right now my browser bookmarks are a cluttered pile, and links also end up
> scattered across notes and open tabs. I often remember what a page was about
> but not its exact title, so finding it again is frustrating.
>
> The "yes, this is exactly what I needed" moment would be saving a link
> quickly, having the useful details filled in for me, and then finding it
> months later by searching a few remembered words or choosing a tag. I'd also
> like to separate things I intend to read from things I'm keeping as a
> reference, and tidy old links away without necessarily deleting them.
>
> So the heart of it is personal saving and rediscovery, rather than sharing
> with other people. I'd like it to feel like a calm, searchable library of
> links, not another overflowing bookmark folder.

### Confirmed direction (facilitator's playback, client-approved)

A calm, personal library of links where the pain of a cluttered browser pile is
replaced by trusting that anything saved can be found again later.

Qualities that define success:

1. Saving is quick, and useful details are filled in automatically rather than
   typed by hand.
2. Rediscovery works from fuzzy memory — find a link later by searching a few
   half-remembered words, or by picking a tag, even without the exact title.
3. Two kinds of links live differently — things intended to read (unread) vs.
   things kept as reference — kept separate rather than mixed in one pile.
   - "Unread" links appear in their own view. Once read, a link leaves that
     view but remains in the library.
4. Tidying away is not deleting — old links can be retired out of sight without
   being lost.
5. It is personal — for the client rediscovering their own things, not sharing.

### Interface form (client-confirmed)

Primarily a browser-based web app used on a computer. A layout that still works
reasonably on a phone is desirable, but no separate phone or desktop app is
needed.
