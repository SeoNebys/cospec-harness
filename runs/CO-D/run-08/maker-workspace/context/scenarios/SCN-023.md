Scenario ID: SCN-023
Title: Recover when a page copy cannot be captured
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-023
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
If a readable page copy cannot be captured, the bookmark remains safely saved with its details and status. A clear message distinguishes the failed copy from the saved bookmark and offers a person-initiated Try again action. A successful retry changes the quiet status line to "Saved with page copy · Open copy" and confirms capture.

## Gherkin
Feature: Recover from failed page capture

  Scenario: Page capture fails while saving a bookmark
    Given a valid bookmark can be saved
    And its readable page copy cannot be captured
    When saving completes
    Then the bookmark remains saved with its details and status
    And the bookmark says its page copy is unavailable
    And a Try again action is available
    And no silent retry is started

  Scenario: Retry page capture successfully
    Given a bookmark's page copy is unavailable
    When the person selects Try again
    And capture succeeds
    Then the bookmark says "Saved with page copy"
    And Open copy is available
    And successful capture is confirmed

## Assumptions
- Page-copy capture is secondary to retaining the bookmark itself.
- Retrying capture requires a person-initiated action.
