Scenario ID: SCN-008
Title: Delete a bookmark deliberately and safely
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-008
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Deletion is available at the bottom of a bookmark's edit form, not on every library card. Choosing it opens a confirmation that names the exact bookmark and explains that its personal note and tags will also be removed. The person can keep the bookmark or confirm deletion. Confirming removes the bookmark and presents an unmistakable deleted and empty-library state.

## Gherkin
Feature: Delete a bookmark

  Scenario: Request deletion from the edit form
    Given the person has deliberately opened a bookmark for editing
    When the person chooses Delete bookmark at the bottom of the form
    Then the app asks for confirmation
    And the confirmation names the exact bookmark
    And the confirmation states that its personal note and tags will also be removed
    And the person can keep the bookmark or confirm deletion

  Scenario: Confirm bookmark deletion
    Given the named delete confirmation is open
    When the person confirms deletion
    Then the bookmark is removed from the library
    And the app shows an unmistakable deletion confirmation
    And an empty library shows a clear empty state

## Assumptions
- Deletion is placed inside the edit form to reduce clutter and accidental requests.
- Deletion requires a separate named confirmation.
- Deleting a bookmark also deletes its attached personal note and tags.
- The tested library contains one bookmark before deletion.
