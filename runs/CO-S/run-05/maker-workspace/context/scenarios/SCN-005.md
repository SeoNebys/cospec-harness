Scenario ID: SCN-005
Title: Archive a finished bookmark without deleting it
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-005
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

The client archives a finished bookmark from its More actions menu. The
bookmark leaves the active collection but remains in a separate Archive view
with its collected page details and labels intact; it is not permanently
deleted.

## Gherkin

Feature: Archiving bookmarks

  Scenario: Tidy a finished bookmark into the archive
    Given an active bookmark with page details and labels is visible
    When the client chooses "Archive" from that bookmark's More actions menu
    Then the bookmark is no longer shown in the active collection
    And the bookmark is shown in the Archive view
    And its page details and labels remain intact

## Assumptions

- Archive is a separate view in the sidebar.
- Archiving is available from a More actions menu rather than as a permanent card button.
- Archiving does not permanently delete a bookmark.
