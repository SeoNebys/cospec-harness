Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a link with an auto-filled, editable title
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
From the main screen the client enters a link address. After they finish
entering the address, the app automatically looks up and fills in a title for
the page, while a brief "looking up…" indication is shown. The client may keep
the auto-filled title or replace it with their own wording; if they edit it,
their version is preserved and not overwritten. The client may also add an
optional short note describing why the link was saved. On saving, the link
appears immediately at the top of the saved-links list (newest first), showing
its title, the site it came from, and the note if one was given. The saved-links
area shows a count of how many links are saved.

## Gherkin
Feature: Saving a link

  Scenario: Title fills in automatically after entering the address
    Given the client is on the main screen
    When the client enters a link address and moves focus away from it
    Then a lookup indication is shown
    And a title for the page is filled into the title field automatically

  Scenario: Keeping the automatically filled title
    Given a link address has been entered and a title auto-filled
    When the client saves the link without changing the title
    Then the link appears at the top of the saved-links list with the auto-filled title
    And the list shows the title, the source site, and the note if provided
    And the saved-links count increases by one

  Scenario: Overriding the automatically filled title
    Given a link address has been entered and a title auto-filled
    When the client edits the title before saving
    Then the client's edited title is preserved and not overwritten by the lookup
    And the saved link shows the client's edited title

  Scenario: Adding an optional reason
    Given the client is saving a link
    When the client types a note about why they are saving it
    Then the saved link displays that note
    And the note may be left empty without preventing the save

## Assumptions
- The save form captures three things: link address, title, optional note.
- Newest saved links appear at the top of the list.
- Each saved link displays title, source site, and note (when present).
- Title lookup happens automatically once the address is entered (simulated in
  the prototype; real fetch to be implemented).
- The link address is required to save; title and note may be adjusted/omitted.
