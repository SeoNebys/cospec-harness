Scenario ID: SCN-040
Title: Apply personal collection display preferences
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The person can choose how many bookmarks appear at once, the default ordering of collection views, and a comfortable text size. Changes are previewed immediately and become reusable defaults after saving. A view may still be reordered temporarily without changing the saved default, and presentation preferences do not alter bookmark content.

## Gherkin
Feature: Personal display preferences

  Scenario: Save collection display defaults
    Given the person is viewing their display preferences
    When they choose 48 bookmarks at once
    And choose Title A–Z as the default order
    And choose Large text
    Then the preview immediately reflects those choices
    When they save the preferences
    Then new collection views use those defaults
    And an individual view can still be reordered temporarily
    And no bookmark content is changed

## Assumptions
- Preferences belong to this single-user collection and persist in the application.
- The saved order is a default, not a restriction on temporary sorting.
