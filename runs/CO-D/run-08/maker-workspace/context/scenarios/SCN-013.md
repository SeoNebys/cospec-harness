Scenario ID: SCN-013
Title: Combine search conditions with grouping and exclusion
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-013
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A search can combine words and #labels with AND, OR, NOT, and parentheses. Parentheses group conditions, OR accepts either grouped condition, AND requires both sides, and NOT excludes the condition immediately after it. Search conditions ignore capitalization.

## Gherkin
Feature: Combine bookmark search conditions

  Scenario: Require text and either label while excluding text
    Given the collection contains bookmarks about Rome with different labels and text
    When the person searches for "rome AND (#article OR #book) AND NOT ancient"
    Then every result mentions Rome
    And every result carries either the article or book label
    And no result mentions ancient

  Scenario: Group an either-or condition
    Given the person places "#article OR #book" in parentheses
    When the search is evaluated
    Then the grouped label condition is evaluated as one part of the surrounding AND conditions

## Assumptions
- NOT applies to the condition immediately after it.
- Condition words and operators ignore capitalization.
