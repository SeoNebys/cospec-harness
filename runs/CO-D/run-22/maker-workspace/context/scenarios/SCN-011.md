Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: Combine text with any of several labels
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A search can combine required ordinary text with multiple labels. By default, the ordinary text must match and a bookmark may carry any one of the listed labels, allowing a broad search across related categories.

## Gherkin
Feature: Searching saved bookmarks

  Scenario: Require text and allow any listed label
    Given the collection contains pasta bookmarks labelled "Cooking" or "Weekend"
    When the person searches for "pasta #cooking #weekend"
    Then every result contains "pasta" in its searchable information
    And every result carries either "Cooking" or "Weekend"
    And bookmarks that do not satisfy both conditions are excluded

## Assumptions
- Ordinary search terms are required.
- Multiple labels listed without explicit operators use OR semantics.
- Capitalization is ignored for both text and labels.
