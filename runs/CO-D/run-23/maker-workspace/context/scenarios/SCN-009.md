Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Permanently delete an unwanted bookmark
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-009
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can choose Delete directly on a bookmark card. Before anything is removed, the app names the exact bookmark, explains that its saved copy will also be removed, and offers both a clear way to keep it and an explicit final deletion action. Confirming permanently removes the bookmark from the library.

## Gherkin
Feature: Delete a bookmark

  Scenario: Confirm permanent deletion
    Given an unwanted bookmark and its preserved copy are in the library
    When the person chooses Delete on its card
    Then the app names the bookmark that would be removed
    And it warns that the saved copy will also be removed
    And it offers actions to keep the bookmark or confirm deletion
    When the person confirms deletion
    Then the bookmark is permanently removed from the library
    And its saved copy is permanently removed

## Assumptions
- Delete is available directly on the bookmark card.
- Deletion is permanent and applies to the bookmark and its preserved content.
- Every deletion requires explicit confirmation.
