Practical Guide to Heirloom Tomatoes

Field Notes Series · Document 14 · Published June 2025

This guide collects growing notes for twelve varieties, with observations from small gardens across three seasons.

Choosing a sunny position

Tomatoes perform best with at least six hours of direct sunlight. Morning light helps dry leaves quickly, while afternoon shade can reduce stress during hot weather.

Keep this document with your seasonal garden notes so you can compare results from year to year.

Watering and feeding

  • Water deeply rather than frequently.
  • Keep moisture away from the leaves.
  • Feed when the first fruit begins to set.

Harvest notes

Pick when the fruit gives slightly under gentle pressure. Color alone is not always a reliable sign of ripeness for green and striped varieties.
