Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Keep crowded bookmark cards scannable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When a bookmark has a very long title, description, note, or many labels, its default card remains compact and similar in size to surrounding cards. Titles and descriptions are visually limited to a few lines without discarding their full saved text. Only the first labels are shown with a clear count of additional labels; they can be expanded and collapsed. Long formatted notes can likewise be expanded in place and collapsed again without changing or flattening their content.

## Gherkin
Feature: Crowded bookmark cards

  Scenario: Compact long saved details
    Given a bookmark has a long title and description
    When its card is shown in the collection
    Then each is visually limited to a compact number of lines
    And the complete saved text remains available when editing

  Scenario: Expand many labels
    Given a bookmark has more labels than its compact card shows
    When the person chooses the additional-label count
    Then all labels appear on the card
    And the person can collapse them again without changing any label

  Scenario: Expand a long formatted note
    Given a bookmark has a note longer than its compact preview
    When the person chooses Show full note
    Then the complete formatted note expands in place
    And choosing Show less restores the compact preview without changing the note

## Assumptions
- Compact cards prioritize scanning a long collection.
- Expansion state changes only presentation, never saved content.
