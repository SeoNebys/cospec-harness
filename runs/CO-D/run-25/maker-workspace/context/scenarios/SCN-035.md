Scenario ID: SCN-035
Title: Prevent duplicate labels with different capitalization
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-035
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Even when existing-label suggestions are bypassed, entering the same label more than once with different capitalization produces one label pill using its established spelling. Label identity does not depend on capitalization.

## Gherkin
Feature: Bookmark labels

  Scenario: Enter an existing label twice with different capitalization
    Given the established label is spelled "Reference"
    When the person enters "reference" without choosing a suggestion
    And enters "REFERENCE" again
    Then exactly one label pill is present
    And it is spelled "Reference"

## Assumptions
- Label-name comparisons ignore capitalization.
