Scenario ID: SCN-017
Title: Cancel permanent deletion without changing anything
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-017
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When the client opens the permanent-deletion warning and chooses Keep bookmark, the warning closes and nothing changes. The bookmark, all of its details, its position in the list, and every section count remain exactly as they were.

## Gherkin

Feature: Permanently delete bookmarks

  Scenario: Back out of permanent deletion
    Given a bookmark is visible in All bookmarks
    And the user has opened its permanent-deletion warning
    When the user chooses "Keep bookmark"
    Then the warning closes
    And the bookmark remains present
    And its saved details remain unchanged
    And its position in the list remains unchanged
    And all section counts remain unchanged

## Assumptions

- Cancelling deletion produces no side effects.
