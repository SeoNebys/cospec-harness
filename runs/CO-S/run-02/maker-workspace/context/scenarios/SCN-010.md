Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Keep unusually long bookmark details readable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
An unusually long bookmark title occupies at most two lines so the collection remains scannable. The full original address may wrap across lines and remains available.

## Gherkin
Feature: Displaying bookmark details

  Scenario: Display unusually long bookmark metadata
    Given a saved bookmark has an unusually long title and address
    When the bookmark is shown in the collection
    Then its visible title occupies no more than two lines
    And its full original address remains available
    And the address wraps rather than overflowing the bookmark card

## Assumptions
- Preserving the full address is more important than forcing the card to a fixed height.
