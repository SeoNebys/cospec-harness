Scenario ID: SCN-006
Title: Reading list — deliberately mark bookmarks to read later
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

A bookmark joins the Reading list only when the client deliberately chooses it —
either by ticking "Add to reading list" in the save/edit review panel, or by the
"Add to reading list" action on the bookmark in the Collection. New saves do NOT
join the reading list automatically. A bookmark that is on the reading list shows
a small "In reading list" marker in the Collection, so its status is visible
without switching views.

"Mark as read" removes a bookmark from the Reading list but keeps it in the
Collection (read = no longer to-read, still an active bookmark). The client can
change a bookmark's reading-list membership later through either the edit panel
or the card action, without removing and re-saving it.

## Gherkin

Feature: Reading list

  Scenario: Add to reading list while saving
    Given I am saving a bookmark
    When I tick "Add to reading list" and save
    Then the bookmark appears in the Reading list
    And it shows an "In reading list" marker in the Collection

  Scenario: Add to reading list later
    Given an active bookmark that is not on the reading list
    When I choose "Add to reading list" on it in the Collection
    Then it appears in the Reading list

  Scenario: New saves do not auto-join the reading list
    When I save a bookmark without choosing to read it later
    Then it appears only in the Collection, not in the Reading list

  Scenario: Mark as read
    Given a bookmark on the Reading list
    When I choose "Mark as read"
    Then it is removed from the Reading list
    But it remains in the Collection

## Assumptions
- Reading-list membership is a deliberate, reversible flag, independent of
  archiving.
