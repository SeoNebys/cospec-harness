Scenario ID: SCN-012
Title: Sign in to a private personal library with email and password
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-012
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The site is a private, single-person library. The client returns by entering their email address and password, then goes directly to their bookmarks. Signing in does not require leaving the site to retrieve a link from email. There are no sharing or other-user management features.

## Gherkin
Feature: Access the private bookmark library

  Scenario: Sign in with email and password
    Given the client is signed out of their private library
    When they enter their correct email address and password
    And submit the sign-in form
    Then their personal bookmark library opens

## Assumptions
- The app serves one client's private collection and has no collaboration or sharing behavior.
- Returning access uses a conventional email-and-password form.
- Successful sign-in goes directly to the library.
