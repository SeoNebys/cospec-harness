Scenario ID: SCN-019
Title: Browse a large collection continuously
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-019
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A large collection initially shows twenty bookmarks. Selecting Show 20 more
appends the next twenty to the same continuous list without removing the items
already visible. The collection states how many bookmarks are currently shown
and the total available. Discrete pages are not used.

## Gherkin
Feature: Browse a large bookmark collection

  Scenario: Reveal another batch of bookmarks
    Given the collection contains 120 bookmarks
    And the first 20 bookmarks are visible
    When the person chooses to show 20 more
    Then the first 20 bookmarks remain visible
    And bookmarks 21 through 40 are appended to the same list
    And the collection states that 40 of 120 bookmarks are shown

## Assumptions
- Bookmarks are revealed in batches of 20.
- The list remains continuous rather than replacing items with discrete pages.
