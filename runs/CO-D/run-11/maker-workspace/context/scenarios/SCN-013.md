Scenario ID: SCN-013
Title: Full content wraps rather than being truncated; responsive layout
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When a bookmark has a very long title, a long note, or many tags, the content
wraps and the card grows to show everything, rather than being cut off with an
ellipsis. Long unbroken text does not spill off the side or break the layout. The
layout is comfortable on a phone-width screen as well as a wide screen; the tag
filter bar also wraps.

## Gherkin
Feature: Content-heavy bookmarks

  Scenario: Long title and note wrap and remain fully visible
    Given a bookmark with a very long title and a long note
    Then the content wraps and the whole card remains readable
    And nothing overflows horizontally, at phone or desktop width

  Scenario: Many tags wrap
    Given a bookmark with many tags
    Then the tags wrap onto multiple lines and the card is not stretched

## Assumptions
- Full content is shown by wrapping/growing, not truncated (client confirmed,
  preferring to see everything over clipping).
