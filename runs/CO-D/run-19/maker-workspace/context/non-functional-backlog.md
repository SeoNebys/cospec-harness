# Non-functional backlog

## Usability and trust

- Prefer plain-language, self-explanatory controls over unexplained icons or punctuation-dependent interactions.
- Keep important state visible through counts, selected states, and brief confirmations.
- Treat destructive actions as deliberate and unmistakable, while letting cancellation leave data untouched.
- Avoid automatic cleanup, expiry, or reshuffling that would make the personal library feel unpredictable.
- Keep large bookmark libraries responsive enough that immediate search and filtering continue to feel immediate; establish measurable targets during development based on the chosen storage and rendering design.

## Readability

- Preserve the skimmable card-based library even when fetched page text or label collections are unusually long.
