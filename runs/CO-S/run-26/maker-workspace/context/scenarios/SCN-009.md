Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark, with confirmation, distinct from archiving
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

The client can permanently delete a bookmark. Delete is a separate action from
archive. Clicking delete does not remove the bookmark immediately; it shows a
confirmation that clearly states the bookmark will be deleted permanently and
cannot be undone, and explicitly points out this is different from archiving,
offering archive as the gentler "set aside" alternative.

Cancelling the confirmation leaves the bookmark untouched. Confirming permanently
removes the bookmark from the app entirely (it does not go to the Archived tab).

## Gherkin

Feature: Permanent delete

  Scenario: Ask for confirmation before deleting
    Given a saved bookmark
    When the client chooses delete
    Then a confirmation is shown stating the deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
    And the confirmation distinguishes delete from archive and offers archive instead
    And the bookmark is not yet removed

  Scenario: Cancel a delete
    Given the delete confirmation is shown
    When the client cancels
    Then the bookmark remains unchanged

  Scenario: Confirm a delete
    Given the delete confirmation is shown
    When the client confirms
    Then the bookmark is permanently removed from the app
    And it does not appear in any tab, including Archived

## Assumptions
- Delete is permanent and irreversible; archive remains the reversible option.
  (Confirmed by client.)
- A confirmation step is required before deletion. (Confirmed by client.)
