Scenario ID: SCN-002
Title: Open a bookmark at its original page
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-002
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Selecting anywhere on a saved bookmark card opens the bookmark's original web page in another browser tab, leaving the personal collection available in its existing tab.

## Gherkin
Feature: Visit saved pages

  Scenario: Open the original page from a bookmark
    Given a bookmark has been saved with its original web address
    When the person selects the saved bookmark card
    Then the original web page opens in another browser tab
    And the bookmark collection remains open in its existing tab

## Assumptions
- The whole bookmark card is selectable.
- The browser permits opening the destination in another tab.
