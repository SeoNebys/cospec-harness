Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Recover honestly when saving cannot gather page details
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-010
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Text that is not a complete HTTP or HTTPS address is blocked from saving, produces no invented details, and receives a clear correction message. When a valid address cannot be reached, the app retains it, explains that details and a readable copy could not be gathered, and allows retrying or adding details manually. The manual form keeps the address and accepts a title, description, and personal note while clearly marking the readable copy as pending. The bookmark can be saved with that context. If a later background attempt captures the readable copy, the app marks it ready without overwriting anything the person entered.

## Gherkin
Feature: Recover from saving failures

  Scenario: Reject text that is not a complete web address
    Given the person is on the bookmark saving screen
    When the person pastes text that is not a complete HTTP or HTTPS address
    Then the app blocks saving
    And the app does not invent page details
    And the app explains how to provide a complete address

  Scenario: Retain a valid address when its page cannot be reached
    Given the person pastes a valid web address
    When the page cannot be reached to gather its details and readable copy
    Then the app keeps the entered address
    And the app explains what could not be gathered
    And the app offers to try again
    And the app offers to add bookmark details manually

  Scenario: Save manual context while a readable copy is pending
    Given a valid page cannot be reached
    When the person adds a title, description, and personal note manually and saves
    Then the bookmark is saved with the entered address and context
    And the app marks the readable copy as pending
    And the app does not claim a readable copy exists

  Scenario: Capture the readable copy later without overwriting manual context
    Given a bookmark was saved with manual context and a pending readable copy
    When the page later becomes reachable and the readable copy is captured
    Then the app marks the readable copy as ready
    And the person's manual title, description, and note remain unchanged

## Assumptions
- Supported saved addresses begin with HTTP or HTTPS.
- An unreachable valid address can still be saved with manual context.
- The app retries pending readable copies after the bookmark is saved.
- A later capture never silently overwrites person-entered context.
