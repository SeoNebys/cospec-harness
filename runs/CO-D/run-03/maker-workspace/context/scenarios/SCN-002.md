Scenario ID: SCN-002
Title: Open a saved item to its actual web page
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When the client finds an item and clicks it, the item's actual web page opens.
The entire result card is clickable (title, image, and body) — the client does
not have to aim at a small link. This is the payoff of rediscovery: found it,
now take me there.

## Gherkin
Feature: Open a saved item

  Scenario: Open by clicking anywhere on the result
    Given a saved item is shown in the collection
    When the client clicks anywhere on that item's card
    Then the item's web page opens

## Assumptions
- The whole card is the click target for opening. (Approved.)
- The Edit control on the card does not trigger opening. (Approved implicitly.)
