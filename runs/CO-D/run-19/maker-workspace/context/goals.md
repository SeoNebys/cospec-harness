# Cycle 1 goal

## Client's words

> Honestly, today my bookmarks are just a messy pile. So a few things I really want:
>
> When I save a link, I don't want to have to type in what it is — I'd like the app to go grab the page's title and description and little icon for me automatically, so my list actually looks like something I can read and skim, not just a wall of raw web addresses.
>
> Then I want to actually *find* things again. Right now I save something and it's gone forever. I'd love to organize with labels I make up, and be able to search across everything and jump right to what I meant.
>
> And a big one: I save stuff meaning to read it later and never do. I'd like a way to mark something as "read this later" and see just those in one place.
>
> That's the heart of it for me — saving should be effortless, and finding things again should just work.

## Confirmation and added context

> Yes, that's the right heart of it. Saving effortless, everything easy to find again, and a "read later" spot that keeps my unfinished reading in front of me — you've got the core.
>
> The one thing I'd add so we're on the same page: it's not just about tidying up while I read. Over time this becomes the place I keep things, so I also care about not ending up with the same link saved twice, and being able to clear out or set aside things I'm done with without losing them by accident. But those can come later — the goal you described is exactly what I'm after.

## Confirmed direction

Saving should be effortless, everything should be easy to find again, and a "read later" spot should keep unfinished reading visible. The app is intended to become the long-term place where bookmarks are kept, so duplicate links and safely clearing out or setting aside finished items must also be explored.

## Confirmed interface

> A web app, I think — something I open in my browser. It's just for me, so I don't need accounts for other people or anything fancy like that. As long as I can get to it and it remembers my links, a website I visit is exactly what I have in mind.

The product will be a personal web app that remembers the client's bookmarks. Multi-user accounts are outside this cycle's goal.
