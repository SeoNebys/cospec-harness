Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: Saved links persist between visits
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Everything the client saves is remembered between visits. After closing the
browser and returning later, all saved links remain, each with its title, note,
tags, reading state, and any kept safety-net copy. Edits and removals are likewise
retained. The client does not have to re-enter anything to see their collection
again.

## Gherkin
Feature: Persistence of saved links

  Scenario: Links remain after leaving and returning
    Given the client has saved links with tags, notes, and reading states
    When the client closes the app and opens it again later
    Then all saved links are still present with their details intact

  Scenario: Edits and removals are retained
    Given the client has edited or removed links
    When the client returns later
    Then those changes are still reflected

## Assumptions
- All bookmark data (address, title, note, tags, reading state, kept copy) is
  stored durably, not just for the current session.
- Single personal user; no login explored in this cycle.
