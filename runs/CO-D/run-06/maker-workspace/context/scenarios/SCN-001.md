Title: Save a bookmark with a chosen title
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can enter a complete web address, optionally provide a title, and save it. The saved link appears in the collection below the save form.

## Gherkin
Feature: Saving bookmarks

  Scenario: Save a bookmark with a chosen title
    Given the bookmark collection is empty
    When the person enters a complete web address and a title and saves the bookmark
    Then the bookmark appears once in the saved-links collection with that title

## Assumptions
- The save form is positioned above the saved-links collection.
- Saving is completed with a visible button.
- Providing a title is optional.
- A newly saved bookmark appears immediately in the collection.
