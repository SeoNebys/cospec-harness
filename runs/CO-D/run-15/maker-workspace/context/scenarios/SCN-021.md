Scenario ID: SCN-021
Title: Load a large collection in manageable batches
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

A large collection initially displays a manageable batch of bookmarks. The client can load another batch into one continuous list without losing their current page position, search words, or selected tag filters. Word search and tag filtering always consider the entire saved collection, including bookmarks whose cards are not currently rendered.

## Gherkin

Feature: Browse a large bookmark collection

  Scenario: Load another batch without disrupting the current view
    Given the collection contains more bookmarks than the initial visible batch
    When the client loads another batch
    Then more bookmark cards are added in place
    And the client's current page position is preserved
    And active search words and tag filters are preserved

  Scenario: Search the whole collection regardless of visible batch
    Given some bookmarks are not in the currently visible batch
    When the client searches by words or filters by tag
    Then every saved bookmark is considered
    And matching does not depend on whether a bookmark card was already visible

## Assumptions

- The collection viewer does not attempt to render every bookmark card at once.
- Additional batches are loaded in place into one continuous list rather than replacing the list with numbered pages.
