Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Group combined search conditions with parentheses
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-016
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Parentheses group combined search conditions. Text outside the parentheses must match together with the grouped result, while alternatives inside the parentheses are evaluated as one unit.

## Gherkin
Feature: Combining bookmark searches

  Scenario: Require text together with either of two labels
    Given the collection contains a Rome article, a Rome book, and a Florence article
    When the person searches for "rome (#article or #book)"
    Then the Rome article and Rome book are shown
    And the Florence article is hidden

## Assumptions
- Conditions beside each other without a written operator narrow results in the same way as "and".
- Parentheses may contain labels, text, and command words.
