Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a link and have its page details filled in automatically
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
From the bookmarks screen, the client pastes a link into the input at the top and
presses Save. The new entry appears immediately in the list while its page details
are being gathered, then fills itself in automatically with: the page title, a
short description, the site's favicon, a preview image, and the site name (host).
The saved entry is shown as a card with the preview image on the left and the
title, description, and site name stacked to the right. The most recently saved
entry appears at the top of the list. The set of collected details (title,
description, favicon, preview image, site name) was confirmed sufficient by the
client, and the card composition/layout was confirmed recognizable.

## Gherkin
Feature: Save a link with automatic page details

  Scenario: Saving a link fills in its page details automatically
    Given the bookmarks screen is open
    When the client enters a link and presses Save
    Then a new entry appears in the list immediately
    And while the details are being gathered the entry shows a fetching state
    And once details are gathered the entry shows the page title, description, favicon, preview image, and site name
    And the entry appears as a card with the preview image on the left and title, description, and site name on the right

  Scenario: The newest saved link appears first
    Given one or more links are already saved
    When the client saves another link
    Then the newly saved entry appears at the top of the list

## Assumptions
- Single-user, personal use.
- Page details are gathered from the link's page at save time (fetch).
- A link entered without a scheme is treated as an https link.
