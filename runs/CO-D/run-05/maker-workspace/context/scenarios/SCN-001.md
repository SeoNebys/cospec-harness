Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Paste a link and it becomes a readable entry
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client pastes a link into a box at the top of the list and presses Save. A brief
"figuring out this page…" state appears, then the link becomes a list entry that shows,
at a glance: a real title on top, a one-line summary beneath it, and the site name (with
a small site icon) below that. The newest saved link appears at the top of the list.

The client confirmed this "reads like a tidy list instead of a wall of web addresses,"
that the amount of information (title + one line + site) is right with nothing more
crammed in, and that newest-at-top matches expectation because the just-saved link is the
one most on their mind.

## Gherkin
Feature: Saving a link

  Scenario: Paste a link and it fills itself in
    Given the bookmark list is open
    When I paste a link into the save box and press Save
    Then a brief "figuring out this page" state is shown
    And the link becomes an entry showing a title, a one-line summary, and the site name
    And the new entry appears at the top of the list

  Scenario: Newest saved link is most prominent
    Given I have one or more saved links
    When I save another link
    Then the newly saved link appears above the previously saved links

## Assumptions
- Each entry displays: title (top), one-line summary, site name + small site icon. No
  additional fields shown.
- Newest entry is inserted at the top of the list.
- Title and summary are derived automatically from the page (real page-reading deferred
  to implementation; prototype simulates it).
