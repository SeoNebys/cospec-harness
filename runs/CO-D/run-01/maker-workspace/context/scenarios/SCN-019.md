Scenario ID: SCN-019
Title: Preserve a bookmark when its original page is unavailable
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
If the original page is temporarily unavailable or permanently gone, attempting to open it can fail without changing the bookmark. The saved title, description, icon, picture, notes, labels, and collection statuses remain untouched so the person can retain their record and try the destination again later.

## Gherkin
Feature: Unavailable original pages

  Scenario: Original page fails to open
    Given a saved bookmark's original page is unavailable
    When the person tries to open the original page
    Then the browser may fail to show the remote page
    And the saved bookmark remains unchanged in the collection
    And all saved details, notes, labels, and statuses are retained

## Assumptions
- Remote-page availability never controls bookmark retention.
- A readable saved copy of remote page content is outside the first-cycle scope and recorded for a later cycle.
