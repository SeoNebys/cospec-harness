Scenario ID: SCN-031
Title: Show a reassuring empty Read later state
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When the final waiting article is marked as read, Read later reaches zero and shows a calm You're all caught up state. The article moves to Finished, the total collection count stays unchanged, and the empty state explicitly reassures the person that finished articles remain saved.

## Gherkin
Feature: Managing a reading queue

  Scenario: Finish the last article in Read later
    Given exactly one article remains in Read later
    When the person marks it as read
    Then Read later shows zero articles
    And a reassuring "You're all caught up" state appears
    And Finished increases by one
    And the total collection count remains unchanged
    And the empty state explains that finished articles are still saved

## Assumptions
- Reaching an empty queue is a successful state, not an error.
