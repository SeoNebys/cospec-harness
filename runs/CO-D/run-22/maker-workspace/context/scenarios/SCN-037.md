Scenario ID: SCN-037
Title: Archive selected bookmarks in bulk with Undo
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can archive all selected bookmarks in the active view with one action. The selected bookmarks leave the view together while retaining their individual information, and a single Undo restores the whole batch exactly as it was.

## Gherkin
Feature: Bulk bookmark organization

  Scenario: Archive every selected bookmark
    Given four visible bookmarks are selected in a filtered view
    When the person chooses the bulk Archive action
    Then all four selected bookmarks move to Archived
    And they leave the active view
    And each retains its own labels, notes, and other information
    And bookmarks outside the selection remain unchanged
    And an Undo action is offered

  Scenario: Undo a bulk archive
    Given a group of bookmarks was just archived together
    When the person chooses Undo
    Then every bookmark in that batch returns to its previous view
    And every bookmark's information remains unchanged

## Assumptions
- Bulk archive has the same reversible semantics as single-bookmark archive.
- One Undo applies to the entire most recent batch.
