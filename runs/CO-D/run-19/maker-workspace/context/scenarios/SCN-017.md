Scenario ID: SCN-017
Title: Keep unusually long bookmark cards skimmable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
An unusually long fetched title and description are trimmed to a consistent compact card for everyday scanning, and only an initial subset of many labels is shown. Show full details expands that card in place to reveal the complete title, description, and remaining labels; Show less returns it to the compact state.

## Gherkin
Feature: Present unusually long bookmark details

  Scenario: View a bookmark with unusually long details
    Given a bookmark has a very long title, description, or many labels
    When it is shown in the everyday library
    Then its card remains compact and consistent with nearby cards
    And a Show full details action is available

  Scenario: Expand and collapse long details
    Given a long bookmark card is compact
    When the client selects Show full details
    Then the complete title, description, and labels are shown in that card
    And a Show less action is available
    When the client selects Show less
    Then the card returns to its compact state

## Assumptions
- Compact display does not remove long text or labels from search.
- Editing always provides the full untrimmed values.
