Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Choose read-later or reference when saving
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The save form offers a "Save to read later" choice, checked by default. When
checked, the new bookmark is saved with status "To read" and appears under both
All and To read. When unchecked (a reference link), the bookmark is saved as
"Finished" and appears under All but not under To read. The choice resets to
checked after each save.

## Gherkin
Feature: Read-later choice at save time

  Scenario: Save as read-later (default)
    Given the "Save to read later" option is checked
    When the client saves a bookmark
    Then the bookmark is created with status "To read"
    And it appears under both "All" and "To read"

  Scenario: Save as reference
    Given the "Save to read later" option is unchecked
    When the client saves a bookmark
    Then the bookmark is created with status "Finished"
    And it appears under "All" but not under "To read"

## Assumptions
- The option defaults to checked and resets to checked after each save
  (client approved).
