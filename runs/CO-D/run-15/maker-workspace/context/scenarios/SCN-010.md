Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Sign in to one private bookmark collection
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

The client signs in with the email and password for one pre-existing private account and lands directly in their bookmark collection. The app does not offer public account registration, user switching, or public profiles.

## Gherkin

Feature: Access a private bookmark collection

  Scenario: Sign in to the private collection
    Given the client has the app's one pre-existing account
    And the client is signed out
    When the client provides the valid email and password
    Then the client is signed in
    And their private bookmark collection is shown
    And no public registration or account-switching flow is offered

## Assumptions

- The app has one private user account.
- Public registration, multiple accounts, account switching, and public profiles are outside scope.
- Sign-in uses email and password rather than a code delivered each time.
