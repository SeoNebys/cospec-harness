Scenario ID: SCN-021
Title: Keep cards scannable when bookmark details are unusually long
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-021
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

An unusually long title is limited to two lines on the collection card, a long description to three lines, and a long address is shortened visually. When a bookmark has many labels, the card shows the first few and a count of the remainder. The card stays aligned with its neighbours. These limits affect presentation only; the complete title, description, address, and every label remain saved and appear in full when editing.

## Gherkin

Feature: Displaying the bookmark collection

  Scenario: Display a bookmark with unusually long details
    Given a bookmark has a very long title, description, address, and eight labels
    When the collection is displayed
    Then its card shows at most two title lines
    And at most three description lines
    And a visually shortened address
    And the first three labels followed by the number of additional labels
    And the card remains aligned with neighbouring cards

  Scenario: Edit details shortened on the card
    Given a card visually shortens long bookmark details
    When the user opens its editor
    Then the complete title is available
    And the complete description is available
    And the complete address is retained
    And every label is retained

## Assumptions

- Card shortening never truncates stored data.
