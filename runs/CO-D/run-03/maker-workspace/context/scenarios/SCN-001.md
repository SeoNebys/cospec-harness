Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a link with automatic capture, then find it again by searching across all its details
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client pastes only a link into the save bar and saves it. The app
automatically captures a title, an image, and a short summary for that link —
the client types nothing else. The saved item appears in the collection with its
image, title, link, and summary.

To rediscover a saved item, the client types a remembered word into a single
search box. The app matches that word across the title, the link, AND the
summary at once (not just the title), showing only the matching items with the
matched word highlighted, and a count of how many of the total matched. The
client confirmed this recovers items whose title does not contain the searched
word (e.g. searching "crust" finds a bread page titled "No-Knead Bread…";
"sleep debt" and "expense ratio" find the right items).

## Gherkin
Feature: Save a link and find it again

  Scenario: Save a link with automatic capture
    Given the client has a link they want to keep
    When the client pastes only the link into the save bar and saves
    Then the app automatically captures a title, an image, and a summary
    And the saved item appears in the collection with those details
    And the client did not have to type anything beyond the link

  Scenario: Find a saved item by a remembered word not in its title
    Given the collection contains a saved item whose summary mentions "crust"
    And that item's title does not contain "crust"
    When the client types "crust" into the single search box
    Then the app shows that item among the results
    And the matched word is highlighted
    And items that match in title, link, or summary are all included

  Scenario: Search narrows the collection
    Given the collection contains several saved items
    When the client types a word that only some items match
    Then only matching items are shown
    And a count of matching-of-total is displayed

## Assumptions
- The starting collection was pre-seeded only to demonstrate; the empty/first-run
  state is not yet designed (deferred to edge-case exploration).
- Search is a single box covering title + link + summary, with no separate
  filters or fields. (Approved.)
- "Just paste, nothing else" capture with no manual entry required. (Approved.)
