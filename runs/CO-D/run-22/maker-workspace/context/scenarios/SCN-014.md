Scenario ID: SCN-014
Title: Group logical search conditions
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can use parentheses to group logical search conditions. Conditions inside a group are evaluated together, while conditions outside the group still apply to the grouped result. Each result explains the combined conditions it satisfied.

## Gherkin
Feature: Precise bookmark search

  Scenario: Group allowed labels while excluding another label
    Given the collection contains pasta bookmarks with labels including "Cooking", "Weekend", and "Travel"
    When the person searches for "pasta AND (#cooking OR #weekend) NOT #travel"
    Then pasta bookmarks carrying "Cooking" or "Weekend" appear
    And bookmarks carrying "Travel" do not appear
    And each result explains that it matched the text, an allowed label, and the exclusion

## Assumptions
- Parentheses determine which logical conditions are evaluated as a group.
- Parenthesized groups can be combined with AND, OR, and NOT.
