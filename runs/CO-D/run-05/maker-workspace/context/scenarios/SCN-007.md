Title: Mark links "to read" and check them off
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
"To read" is a *state* a link is in (waiting-to-read vs. read), not a label. A distinct
"📖 To read" entry sits apart from the labels in the bar, with a count, and pulls up just
the links the client means to get to ("N to go" — checklist energy).

Getting a link into the pile, two ways: a "Add it to my 'to read' pile" checkbox at save
time, and a small "📖 To read" button on any not-yet-flagged link later. The save-time
checkbox does NOT default on (the client was firm: if everything auto-landed in to-read,
the pile would become "everything saved" and lose meaning — "a pile I can never empty is
just stressful"). The checkbox is sticky: it remembers the client's last choice across
saves, so a stretch of stashing reading material needs one tick, and filing needs one
untick.

Checking off: a circle on the left of each row. Ticking it marks the link read — it slides
out of the to-read pile and the count drops, but the link stays saved, labelled, and
findable, now showing a quiet "✓ Read". Ticking the green circle again returns it to the
pile (undo matters — the client will misfire or change their mind). The "✓ Read" marker
stays visible on purpose: "done, but kept" is shown on the face of it.

## Gherkin
Feature: Read-later pile

  Scenario: View the to-read pile
    Given some links are marked to read
    When I open the "To read" view
    Then only links marked to read are shown, with a count

  Scenario: Check off a link
    Given I am viewing the to-read pile
    When I tick a link's circle
    Then it leaves the pile and the count decreases
    And the link is still saved and findable, now marked "Read"

  Scenario: Undo a check-off
    Given a link marked read
    When I tick its circle again
    Then it returns to the to-read pile

  Scenario: Save straight into the pile, sticky choice
    Given the "add to to-read" checkbox reflects my last choice
    When I save a link
    Then the link's to-read state matches the checkbox
    And the checkbox keeps that setting for my next save

## Assumptions
- Save-time to-read defaults off; sticky per session.
- Read links keep a visible "✓ Read" marker rather than reverting to look untouched.
