Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Search ranks title/summary hits above body-only hits, and copes with several half-remembered words
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Results are ranked so the most obvious matches lead. A match in the title,
summary, or a label is "strong" and always ranks ABOVE a match found only inside
the saved page content. A visible divider separates the strong matches from the
body-only matches ("matched only inside the page contents — further down on
purpose").

Each result explains WHY it matched — e.g. "bread — in title" vs "bread — in page
text" — and body-only matches show a short snippet of where the word appears, so
unexpected results are legible and trust is built.

Multiple typed words are combined loosely: EVERY word must appear somewhere, but
the words may be scattered across title, summary, labels, or page text and need
NOT be adjacent or co-located. (The everyday "throw a couple of half-remembered
words at it" case.)

## Gherkin
Feature: Search ranking and loose multi-word matching

  Scenario: Title match ranks above body-only match
    Given one item has the term in its title and another only in its body
    When the client searches that term
    Then the title match is listed above the body-only match
    And a divider marks where body-only matches begin

  Scenario: Explain why each result matched
    When results are shown
    Then each shows which field(s) matched, and body matches show a snippet

  Scenario: Loose multi-word search
    Given the client types two words
    When both words each appear somewhere in an item (any field), not necessarily together
    Then that item matches

## Assumptions
- Field weighting title/summary/labels > body; divider between. (Approved.)
- "Why matched" chips + body snippet shown. (Approved — builds trust.)
- Default multi-word semantics = AND across any field, no adjacency. (Approved.)
