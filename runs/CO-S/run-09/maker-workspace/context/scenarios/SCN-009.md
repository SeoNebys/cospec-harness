Scenario ID: SCN-009
Title: Revisit a saved readable copy when the original page disappears
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-009
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The app captures a readable copy of an article when the bookmark is first saved. If the original page later becomes unavailable, the bookmark card clearly identifies that the original cannot be reached and that a saved copy is ready. Clicking the card through the usual reading action automatically opens the saved copy in a new browser tab while preserving the library in its original tab. The saved copy contains the title, description, and readable article body rather than only metadata.

## Gherkin
Feature: Preserve saved content

  Scenario: Capture a readable copy when saving
    Given the person is saving an available article page
    When the bookmark is saved
    Then the app captures a readable copy containing the article title, description, and body

  Scenario: Open a saved copy after the original disappears
    Given the library contains a bookmark with a captured readable copy
    And the original page can no longer be reached
    When the person views the bookmark
    Then the card states that the original page is unavailable
    And the card states that a saved copy is ready
    When the person clicks the bookmark card
    Then the saved readable copy opens in a new browser tab
    And the library remains in place in the original tab

## Assumptions
- Readable copies are captured automatically when a bookmark is first saved.
- A readable copy includes the article body, not just bookmark metadata.
- The app can identify that an original page is unavailable.
- The usual card-click reading action automatically falls back to the saved copy.
- The fallback copy opens in a new tab.
