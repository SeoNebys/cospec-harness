Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Mark a bookmark "to read", view the to-read pile, and check it off as read
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
"To read" is a STATUS, kept separate from topic labels. It is presented apart
from the Labels row (its own control up top, styled distinctly) so status and
topic are never confused.

- Flag: one click on a card marks an item "to read" (no editor, no label needed);
  the card shows a distinct "to read" indicator.
- Pile: a dedicated "To read" view shows only unread items, with a count of how
  many are waiting.
- Check off: one click marks an item read; it drops out of the to-read pile
  immediately, while the bookmark itself remains in the collection. An Undo is
  offered so the client isn't afraid to click.

## Gherkin
Feature: Reading queue (to-read status)

  Scenario: Flag an item to read
    Given a bookmark that is not marked to read
    When the client clicks the "to read" control on its card
    Then the bookmark is marked to read
    And it appears in the to-read pile and the pile count increases

  Scenario: View the to-read pile
    When the client opens the "To read" view
    Then only unread bookmarks are shown, with a count waiting

  Scenario: Check an item off as read
    Given the client is viewing the to-read pile
    When the client marks an item as read
    Then it drops out of the to-read pile immediately
    And the bookmark remains in the overall collection
    And an Undo is offered

  Scenario: Flag "to read" while saving
    Given the client is saving a link they have no time to read now
    When the client ticks the "To read" option beside Save and saves
    Then the new bookmark is placed directly in the to-read pile

## Assumptions
- "To read" is a status separate from topic labels, shown apart. (Approved.)
- One click to flag and one click to clear, on the card. (Approved.)
- Flagging "to read" at the moment of saving via a checkbox beside Save, decided
  in the same motion as saving. (Approved.)
