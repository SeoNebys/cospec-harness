Scenario ID: SCN-008
Title: Archive a bookmark (tuck away but keep) — distinct from delete-forever
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Archiving is a status meaning "done for now, but don't lose it" — distinct from
both deleting (gone for good) and the to-read pile.

- One click on a card's Archive control tucks an item away (same easy feel as the
  to-read flag), and it slides out of the everyday list.
- Archived items are hidden from the everyday list, the to-read pile, the label
  groups, AND from everyday search — fully out of the way. (The client
  explicitly confirmed archived items must NOT surface in normal search.)
- A dedicated Archived view lists archived items, each with a Restore control to
  bring it back to the everyday collection.
- To keep the distinction plain, the destructive action is labelled
  "Delete forever" (archive = keep, delete = gone).

## Gherkin
Feature: Archive vs delete

  Scenario: Archive an item
    Given an item in the everyday collection
    When the client archives it
    Then it is removed from the everyday list, to-read pile, label groups, and search
    And it remains available in the Archived view

  Scenario: Archived items do not appear in everyday search
    Given an archived item
    When the client searches from the everyday list
    Then the archived item is not shown

  Scenario: Restore an archived item
    Given an item in the Archived view
    When the client restores it
    Then it returns to the everyday collection

## Assumptions
- Archive is a one-click card action; Archived is a third view alongside
  All bookmarks and To read. (Approved.)
- Archived items excluded from everyday search. (Approved explicitly.)
