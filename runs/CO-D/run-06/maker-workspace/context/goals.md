# Business goal statement

_Recorded in the client's language during Phase 1, Step 1 (Cycle 1)._

## The real goal

One place to save links the moment I find them (at my desk, in my browser), so I
**stop losing things** I meant to come back to. Today links are scattered across
browser bookmarks, emails to myself, and forever-open tabs, and when I want one
back I can't find it.

Both **saving** and **getting back to them** must be effortless.

## Getting back to things — two first-class paths

Neither is a fallback for the other. Both matter on their own.

1. **Browse by label** — label things, then pull up "everything filed under
   recipes" and just look through that pile. Labeling and browsing-by-label are
   valued for their own sake, not only as a search aid.
2. **Search** — not a dumb keyword box. It looks *inside* everything saved
   (title, my notes, description, web address — all of it), and lets me
   **combine** conditions, e.g. "recipes, but only the ones tagged dinner."

## Effortless saving (emphasised by client)

When I paste a link I don't want to type out the title and description myself.
The app should go grab that from the page and pre-fill title + a short
description. I can tweak if it's wrong, but the app does the first pass. This is
a big part of what "takes a second" means.

## Recorded aspiration — keeping a copy (raised late in Cycle 1)

The deepest form of "stop losing things": the client wants the app to keep its own
saved copy (snapshot) of each page as it was at save time, so the content survives
even if the original site dies. Paired with personal notes, a dead bookmark keeps
all its value. Client's words: "the truest answer to why I'm building this at all."
Deferred from v1 by the client only if it proves genuinely large; must not be
dropped silently. Tracked as parking-lot P3.

## Interface form

A **web app** opened in a desktop browser. Sitting-at-my-desk use.
Phone / mobile is out of scope for now.

## Client's own words (verbatim highlights)

- "I'm constantly finding links I want to come back to ... right now they're
  scattered everywhere."
- "when I actually want to find something again, I can't."
- "Saving needs to be dead simple or I won't do it."
- "I like being able to see related stuff together — like 'show me everything I
  filed under recipes' and just browse that pile."
- "when I search I'd want it to actually look inside everything ... the titles,
  my own notes, the descriptions, the web address, all of it."
- "'recipes but only the ones I tagged dinner.'"
- "I don't want to sit there typing out what the page is called ... it should go
  grab that stuff from the page itself."
