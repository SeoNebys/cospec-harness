Scenario ID: SCN-014
Title: Show an honest zero-result search state
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When no bookmarks satisfy the active word search and tag filter, the app clearly shows that zero bookmarks match. It leaves the client's search words and filter selection in place and keeps the controls available to change. It does not broaden, guess at, or substitute different results.

## Gherkin

Feature: Rediscover saved bookmarks

  Scenario: No bookmarks match the active search criteria
    Given the client has entered search words and may have selected a tag filter
    And no bookmark satisfies all active criteria
    When the results are displayed
    Then the app shows that zero bookmarks match
    And the active search words and tag filter remain unchanged
    And the search and filter controls remain available
    And the app does not broaden or substitute the search results

## Assumptions

- Clearing or changing the search and filters uses the existing controls.
