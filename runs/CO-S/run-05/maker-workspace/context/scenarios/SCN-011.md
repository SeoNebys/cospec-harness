Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: Show an understandable empty archive
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-011
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When no bookmarks have been archived, the Archive sidebar view remains open
and explains that it is empty. It does not redirect the client or appear
broken, making it clear that the archive is available and waiting for items.

## Gherkin

Feature: Archiving bookmarks

  Scenario: Open the archive before anything is archived
    Given no bookmarks have been archived
    When the client opens the Archive view
    Then the Archive view remains open
    And it explains that nothing has been archived yet

## Assumptions

- An empty focused view does not redirect to another view.
