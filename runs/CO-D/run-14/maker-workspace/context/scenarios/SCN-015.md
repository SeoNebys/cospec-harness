Scenario ID: SCN-015
Title: Recover from an incorrect password without revealing account details
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-015
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When sign-in fails, the client remains signed out and sees a deliberately general message that does not reveal whether the email or password was wrong. The entered email remains filled in, while the password is cleared and ready for another attempt. Entering the correct password afterward opens the private library normally.

## Gherkin
Feature: Recover from failed sign-in

  Scenario: Reject incorrect credentials without identifying the incorrect field
    Given the client is on the sign-in page
    When they submit an incorrect password
    Then they remain signed out
    And a general email-or-password error is shown
    And the message does not confirm whether the email belongs to an account
    And the email remains filled in
    And the password field is cleared

  Scenario: Retry successfully after a failed sign-in
    Given a failed sign-in message is visible
    When the client enters the correct password and signs in again
    Then their private library opens

## Assumptions
- Sign-in errors never distinguish an unknown email from an incorrect password.
- Retaining the email is acceptable, but retaining an incorrect password is not.
