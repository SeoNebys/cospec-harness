# Cycle 1 goal

## Client statement

> Honestly, today my bookmarks are a mess — they're scattered, and when I save one it's just a bare link with no context, so later I can't tell what it was or find it again.
>
> What I'd want to be easier:
>
> - **Saving is effortless and smart.** I paste a link and the app fills in the title, description, and a little icon/preview on its own, so I don't have to type all that. And if I save something I already saved before, it shouldn't make a duplicate — it should just let me update the one I have.
> - **Finding things again is quick.** I want to organize with tags and then search properly — search that looks through titles, descriptions, and notes, not just exact link text.
> - **I can revisit and manage them.** Edit a bookmark, add my own notes, delete ones I don't want, and mark things as "read later" so I can come back to them.
> - **Nothing gets lost.** I'd like some way that even if a page disappears later, I still have what I saved.
>
> The main thing overall: turn a pile of random links into something I can actually organize, search, and come back to. Those are the parts that matter most to me.

## Confirmed direction

> Yes, that's exactly the right direction. You've captured it well — effortless saving, no duplicates, and being able to find and revisit anything later even if the original page is gone. That's the heart of what I want.

## Interface form

> A web app would suit me best — something I can open in my browser on my computer. That's where I do most of my saving and reading anyway.
