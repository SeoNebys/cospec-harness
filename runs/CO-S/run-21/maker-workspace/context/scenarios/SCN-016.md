Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Permanently delete a bookmark with confirmation
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-016
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Permanent deletion is available from a bookmark's overflow menu and is clearly distinct from Archive. Choosing it opens a confirmation that names the exact bookmark, explains that its note, tags, and saved details will also be removed, and states that the action cannot be undone. The person must deliberately confirm a second time before removal. Confirmation is preferred over immediate deletion with a brief Undo period.

## Gherkin
Feature: Permanently remove a bookmark deliberately

  Scenario: Confirm permanent deletion
    Given a saved bookmark has details, tags, and a personal note
    When the person opens the bookmark's overflow menu
    And chooses "Delete permanently"
    Then a confirmation names the exact bookmark
    And explains that its note, tags, and saved details will be removed
    And explains that the deletion cannot be undone
    When the person confirms "Delete permanently" a second time
    Then the bookmark and its associated information are permanently removed

## Assumptions
- Permanent deletion is not recoverable through Archive.
- Cancelling the confirmation leaves the bookmark unchanged.
