Scenario ID: SCN-014
Title: Move a bookmark to Archive without deleting it
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-014
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The occasional "Move to Archive" action is kept in the bookmark's overflow menu. Using it removes the bookmark from All bookmarks, increases the Archive count, and plainly confirms that the bookmark has been kept safe. Archiving is reversible and explicitly distinct from permanent deletion.

## Gherkin
Feature: Tidy the active collection without losing bookmarks

  Scenario: Archive a bookmark from its overflow menu
    Given a bookmark is in All bookmarks
    When the person opens that bookmark's overflow menu
    And chooses "Move to Archive"
    Then the bookmark is removed from All bookmarks
    And the Archive count increases by one
    And the app explains that the bookmark is kept safe
    And the bookmark is not permanently deleted

## Assumptions
- Archived bookmarks are excluded from All bookmarks.
- Archiving does not discard bookmark details, tags, or notes.
- Archive is an occasional action placed in the overflow menu.
