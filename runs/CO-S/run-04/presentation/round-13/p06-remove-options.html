Removing a link — two ways

Same links in both. Hover a link to reveal its trash (🗑) control. The panels differ only in what protects you from deleting by accident.

Option A — Ask first

Click the trash and the row asks you to confirm ("Remove this link?") with Remove / Cancel before anything is deleted.

    Option B — Remove, with Undo

    Click the trash and the link is removed right away, but a short "Removed — Undo" message lets you bring it back if it was a mistake.
