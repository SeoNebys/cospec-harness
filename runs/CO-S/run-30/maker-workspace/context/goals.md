# Cycle 1 goal

## Client statement

> Right now my bookmarks are kind of a mess — they're scattered, and when I save a link it's just a bare address with no real title, so later I can't tell what any of them were. What I really want is:
>
> - When I paste a link, the app should grab the page's title and a bit of description automatically, so I'm not typing it all in myself.
> - A way to organize them with my own labels (tags), and then filter down to just the ones with a certain label.
> - Real search that actually looks inside the titles and descriptions, not just exact matches — so I can find something even when I only half-remember it.
> - Some notion of "I'll read this later" versus stuff I've already gone through.
> - And honestly, just a list that's pleasant to skim, where I click an item and it takes me to the actual page.
>
> If I ever save the same link twice, I'd rather it just let me update the one I already have instead of piling up duplicates. That's the heart of it for me.

## Confirmation and additions

> Yes, that's the right direction. You've captured it well.
>
> The only thing I'd add so we're on the same page: when I search, I want it to be forgiving — finding things even if my capitalization is off, and letting me narrow by a label as part of searching, not just plain word matching. And beyond "read later," I'd like a way to tuck older bookmarks out of the way without deleting them, so my main list stays clean but I can still get them back later.
>
> But yes — a pleasant, searchable library that describes pages for me, uses my own labels, tracks what I still need to read, avoids duplicates, and opens the real page. That's exactly what I'm after.

## Interface

> A web app, please — something I can open in my browser on my computer. That's where I do most of my link-saving anyway. It's just for me, so I don't need anything fancy for other people or phones right now.
