Scenario ID: SCN-012
Title: Archive a bookmark (hide from collection & search) and restore it
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Archiving is a holding area separate from read/finished status. From a bookmark's
More menu, "Archive" removes it from the active collection: it no longer appears
in All / To read / Finished views or in search, but it is not deleted. Archiving
does not change whether the bookmark is To read or Finished.

The Archived view is reached via a separate "🗄 Archived (n)" link at the right of
the controls (kept distinct from the To read / Finished switch, which is hidden
while viewing the archive). The link shows the count of archived items and toggles
to "← Back to collection". In the Archived view, each bookmark offers Restore
(returns it to the active collection with its previous status intact) and, under
More, permanent Delete. When nothing is archived, the view explains it is a
holding area kept out of the main collection.

The client confirmed: the separate link (Option A) over an extra switch;
archiving leaves read-status unchanged; archived items stay out of normal views
and searches; and restore/permanent-delete from the archive.

## Gherkin
Feature: Archive

  Scenario: Archive a bookmark
    When I choose Archive from a bookmark's More menu
    Then it is removed from the active collection and from search results
    But it is not deleted
    And its To read / Finished status is unchanged
    And the Archived link count increases

  Scenario: View and restore from the archive
    When I open the Archived view
    Then I see only archived bookmarks
    And the To read / Finished switch is hidden
    When I Restore a bookmark
    Then it returns to the active collection with its previous status

  Scenario: Permanently delete from the archive
    Given I am viewing the archive
    When I choose Delete for an archived bookmark and confirm
    Then it is permanently removed

## Assumptions
- Archive and read-status are independent dimensions.
- Search and the normal views operate only on active (non-archived) bookmarks.
- The Archived view is opened from a dedicated link, not the status switch.
