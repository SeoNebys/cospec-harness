Scenario ID: SCN-020
Title: Keep a bookmark when its website becomes unavailable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-020
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
If a website becomes unavailable after its bookmark was saved, the app does not
remove or alter the bookmark automatically. Its title, description, labels, and
address remain intact. The person remains in control and can correct the
address, archive the bookmark, or permanently delete it.

## Gherkin
Feature: Preserve saved bookmarks over time

  Scenario: A saved website becomes unavailable
    Given a bookmark has stored details and an address
    When the external website later becomes unavailable
    Then the bookmark remains in the collection
    And its title, description, labels, and address remain unchanged
    And the person can edit, archive, or delete it
    And the app does not remove it automatically

## Assumptions
- External website availability never determines whether a bookmark is retained.
- The app does not automatically rewrite saved details when a site becomes unavailable.
