Scenario ID: SCN-037
Title: Preserve personal data and reset temporary viewing context
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-037
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Closing and reopening the app preserves every saved bookmark, edited detail, personal note, label, Read later status, and the person's selected default sort order. Temporary viewing context—search text, an active label filter, and an expanded long note—clears, and the app reopens on the full collection.

## Gherkin
Feature: Returning to the bookmark collection

  Scenario: Reopen the app after personalizing the collection
    Given the person has saved and edited bookmarks
    And added notes, labels, and Read later status
    And selected a sort order
    And currently has a search, label filter, and expanded note open
    When the person closes and later reopens the app
    Then all bookmark data and Read later status remain
    And the selected sort order remains
    And the app shows the full collection
    And no prior search or label filter is active
    And long notes are collapsed

## Assumptions
- The app is a personal collection with no multi-user account flow in this cycle.
