# Cycle 1 goal

## Client statement

> Right now my bookmarks are kind of a mess — I've got them scattered around and it's hard to find anything later. So the main thing I want is a single place where I can quickly save a link and actually find it again when I need it.
>
> A few things that would make my life easier:
>
> - When I paste in a web address, I'd love it to grab the page's title and a bit of info automatically, so I'm not typing all that out myself. I can tweak it if it gets it wrong.
> - Being able to put labels or tags on things, and then filter down to just one kind of bookmark.
> - A good search — I want to type a word and find the link even if I only remember part of it.
> - A way to mark things as "read this later" and see just those.
> - And when I click a saved item, it should take me to the actual website.
>
> Some other nice-to-haves down the road: keeping my own notes on a bookmark, tidying old ones out of the way without deleting them, and being able to bring in the bookmarks I already have saved elsewhere.
>
> But the heart of it is: save a link easily, and find it again without hunting.

## Confirmed direction

> Saving a link with its details filled in, and reliably finding it again through search, tags, or a "read later" view, with a click taking me to the original site — that's exactly the heart of it.
>
> And yes, personal notes, putting old bookmarks out of sight, and importing what I already have can come later.

## Confirmed interface

> A web app would suit me best — something I can open in my browser on my computer. That way I don't have to install anything, and I can get to my bookmarks wherever I am. It's just for me, so nothing fancy with accounts for other people.

## In-scope management refinement

> One thing I haven't seen yet that I'd expect as part of "managing" my bookmarks: once something's saved, I'd want to be able to tidy it up — fix a title or tags, or remove a link I no longer want.
