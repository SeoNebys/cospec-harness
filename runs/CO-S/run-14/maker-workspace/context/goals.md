# Business goal

## Cycle 1 — recorded SESSION-001 (2026-09-23)

## Core goal (client's words)

> The main goal is reliable capture and retrieval — being able to save
> something quickly and actually find and understand it later.

Today the client saves links in different browser folders, then struggles to
find them later or forgets why they were saved. They want **one personal place**
to solve this.

## Supporting needs (in service of the core, not separate purposes)

- Save a link quickly
- Have its basic details filled in automatically
- Add tags or a note (to capture *why* it was saved)
- Find links later through search or filters
- Mark things to "read later"
- Archive links to keep them without cluttering the main list

The client confirmed read-later and archiving **support** reliable capture and
retrieval rather than being separate purposes.

## Scope

- Personal, single-user app. No team sharing required.

## Interface form

- Browser-based website (web app). Reason (client's words): "so I can use it
  from any computer without installing a separate app."
