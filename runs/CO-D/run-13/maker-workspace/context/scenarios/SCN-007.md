Title: Review and correct auto-filled details before finalizing a save
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

Saving a link is a brief two-step flow: fetch, then review, then add.

When the client enters an address and presses Save, the system fetches the
page's details and shows a review panel pre-filled with the auto-filled Title
and Description (both editable), the address, and an optional note field. The
client can correct the details, then press "Add to library" to finalize, or
"Discard" to back out without saving anything.

- If the fetched page details could not be read, the review panel says so and
  invites the client to fill them in.
- If the address is already saved (SCN-002 identity), no review is shown; the
  client is taken to the existing entry instead.
- The entry is created only when the client confirms "Add to library"; it then
  appears at the top of the list, briefly highlighted (SCN-001).

The client approved the review step as worth the small extra effort: it is still
quick and prevents poor auto-filled details from becoming something to fix later.
"Discard" makes backing out clear.
