Scenario ID: SCN-014
Title: Open a bookmark's page, and filter the current view by a clicked tag
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

Clicking a bookmark's title or its preview image opens the original page in a new
browser tab. The buttons, the tags, the plain address link, and links inside the
note keep their own behaviour (the whole card is deliberately not one click
target).

Clicking a tag on a card filters by that exact tag, applied through the visible
search box (shown as #tag, or #"multi word tag" for tags with spaces) so it stays
visible and adjustable. The filter respects the current view: clicking a tag in
the Collection filters the Collection, in the Reading list filters the Reading
list, and in the Archive filters the Archive — it never moves the client to a
different view.

## Gherkin

Feature: Opening and tag-filtering

  Scenario: Open the original page
    When I click a bookmark's title or preview image
    Then its original page opens in a new browser tab

  Scenario: Filter the current view by a clicked tag
    Given I am viewing the Collection, Reading list, or Archive
    When I click a tag on one of its cards
    Then the current view is filtered to bookmarks carrying that exact tag
    And the search box shows the tag filter, remaining visible and adjustable
    And I stay in the same view

## Assumptions
- Tag-click filtering reuses the exact-tag search (#tag); tags with spaces use the
  quoted form #"...".
