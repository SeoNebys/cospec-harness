# Cycle 1 goal

## Confirmed goal

Turn the bookmark hoard into something the client can actually find and work
through: saved links capture the page's title and a bit of information
automatically; labels or tags group them by topic; search finds buried bookmarks
and their tags; a "read later" view keeps the reading pile visible; and finished
items can be tidied away without being deleted forever.

## Client statements

> Right now my bookmarks are kind of a mess. I save links in my browser and then never find them again — they're just a long list with no real organization, and searching is basically useless.
>
> So what I'm hoping for:
>
> - When I save a link, I'd love it to just *grab* the page's title and a bit of info automatically, so I don't have to type anything to remember what it was.
> - Being able to put labels or tags on things, so I can group them by topic and pull up just the ones I want.
> - A search that actually works — where I can type a word and find the bookmark even if it's buried, and ideally search by those tags too.
> - A way to mark something as "I'll read this later" and see just those, so my reading pile doesn't vanish into the whole list.
> - And when I'm done with something, tidy it away without necessarily deleting it forever.
>
> Basically I want to actually *find and revisit* what I saved, instead of hoarding links I never look at again. Does that give you enough to start with?

> Yes, that's exactly it. You've captured the heart of what I want — turning that hoard into something I can actually find and work through. That's the right goal.

## Confirmed interface

A web app opened in the client's computer browser, where they do most of their
saving and reading.

> A web app would suit me best — something I can just open in my browser on my computer. That's where I do most of my saving and reading anyway, so having it all there in one place is what I have in mind.
