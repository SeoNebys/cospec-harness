Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a new link quickly and have it stored with title and summary
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client pastes (or types) a link into the save box at the top of the screen
and presses Save. The link is stored immediately and appears at the top of the
saved list with a brief highlight. The app fills in, on its own, the page's real
title (the same words shown in the browser tab) and a one-line summary of what
the page is about, plus the site's icon. The client does not have to type a
title. While the details are being looked up, the entry briefly shows a
"fetching details" state, then settles into the finished card.

## Gherkin
Feature: Save a link

  Scenario: Saving a new link stores it with auto-filled details
    Given the client is on the bookmarks screen
    When the client enters a link and presses Save
    Then the link is stored and shown at the top of the saved list
    And the app fills in the page's own title without the client typing it
    And the app fills in a one-line summary of what the page is about
    And the site's icon is shown next to the link

  Scenario: A link without a leading scheme is still accepted
    Given the client is on the bookmarks screen
    When the client enters a link that omits "http://" or "https://"
    Then the link is still stored and treated as a normal web address

## Assumptions
- The title and summary are obtained from the page itself (server-side in the
  real app; simulated in the Phase 1 prototype).
- The site icon (favicon) is fetched from the site; falls back to a colored
  initial when unavailable (favicon fallback is NF-1, visual only).
- Newest saved link appears at the top of the list.
- Saved-date is shown but is low-priority (NF-2).
