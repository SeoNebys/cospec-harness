Scenario ID: SCN-027
Title: Preserve a bookmark when its original webpage changes or disappears
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-027
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

After a bookmark is saved, its stored title, description, labels, and other bookmark details remain under the user's control. If the original webpage later changes or becomes unavailable, the app does not silently overwrite or delete the saved bookmark. Opening the bookmark still attempts to visit its original address, even if that address now returns an error.

## Gherkin

Feature: Preserving saved bookmark records over time

  Scenario: The original webpage changes after being bookmarked
    Given a bookmark has been saved with its title, description, and labels
    When the original webpage later changes
    Then the saved bookmark details remain unchanged
    And the app does not silently replace the user's saved wording

  Scenario: The original webpage becomes unavailable after being bookmarked
    Given a bookmark has been saved
    When the original webpage later becomes unavailable
    Then the bookmark remains in the collection with all its saved details
    And opening it still attempts to visit the original address

## Assumptions

- Automatically preserving a readable copy of the webpage itself is explicitly deferred to a later cycle.
- This cycle does not perform ongoing page availability checks or automatic metadata refreshes.
