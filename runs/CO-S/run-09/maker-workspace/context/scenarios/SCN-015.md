Scenario ID: SCN-015
Title: Cancel deletion without changing the bookmark
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-015
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When the named delete confirmation is open and the person chooses Keep bookmark, the warning closes and the unchanged edit form returns. The bookmark and all of its details remain intact; cancellation has no deletion or editing side effects.

## Gherkin
Feature: Delete a bookmark

  Scenario: Cancel bookmark deletion
    Given the named delete confirmation is open for a bookmark
    When the person chooses Keep bookmark
    Then the delete confirmation closes
    And the unchanged bookmark edit form is shown
    And the bookmark remains in the library with all of its details intact

## Assumptions
- Keep bookmark is the non-destructive action in the delete confirmation.
- Cancelling deletion does not discard unsaved form content or modify stored bookmark content.
