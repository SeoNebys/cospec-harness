# Cycle 1 goal

## Client statement

> Honestly, right now my bookmarks are a mess. I save links in my browser and then never find them again — there's no real way to search through them or make sense of what I've got.
>
> So the big thing for me: when I paste in a link, I'd love the app to just *figure out* what the page is on its own — grab the title, a little description, the site's icon, maybe a preview image — so I'm not typing all that in by hand. And then I want to be able to actually find things later: search by words, and organize with my own labels or tags so I can pull up "all my cooking links" or whatever in one click.
>
> A few other things that frustrate me today: if I save the same link twice by accident, I don't want two copies cluttering things up — it should just take me to the one I already have. And I've got this pile of "I'll read this later" links that I never get back to, so being able to flag those and see just that list would be great.
>
> The only thing I'd add so we're on the same page: "easy to rediscover" is a bit bigger for me than just a plain search box. I want to be able to search the words in a link, but also filter down by my tags, and combine those — like "show me my travel links about Rome." And when I look at my list, I want it to be readable at a glance: the title, the little description, the tags, the site icon, all right there, and clicking one just takes me to the actual page.
>
> But yes — organize itself on paste, no duplicates, find things through search and tags, and a focused read-later list. That's the right direction.

## Confirmed direction

Organize itself on paste, no duplicates, find things through search and tags, and a focused read-later list. “Easy to rediscover” includes combining words and tags, and presenting the title, little description, tags, and site icon at a glance; clicking a saved item takes the client to the actual page.

## Interface form

> I'm picturing a web app, really — something I open in my browser on my computer. That's where I do most of my saving and reading anyway. It'd be nice if the page wasn't broken on my phone, but I'm not asking for a separate mobile app. Just a website I can log into and use. It's only me using it, so nothing fancy with multiple accounts.

Confirmed as a web app used primarily in a computer browser, for one person. A separate mobile app is outside the goal.
