Scenario ID: SCN-010
Title: Flexible label combining — narrow (all), widen (any), and exclude (not)
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Beyond the default AND narrowing (SCN-003), the client can widen or exclude, with
the extra power kept out of the everyday case's way.

- Default: selecting labels narrows (AND / "all of these"). Unchanged for the common
  case.
- All/Any switch: when 2 or more labels are included, a small "all of these / any of
  these" switch appears next to them. "Any of these" widens to bookmarks with ANY of
  the selected labels (OR). With only one label selected, the switch does not appear
  (nothing to combine); it defaults to "all".
- Exclude (NOT): every label in the sidebar carries a small "–" marker that is always
  visible but quiet (faint grey), becoming red on hover. Clicking it excludes the
  label, hiding bookmarks that carry it. Excluded labels show as red "not X" chips.
- The active-filter bar reads like a sentence, e.g. "recipes · not dinner". Mixing
  include (all/any) and exclude in one bar is clear because the red "not" chips make
  exclusion obvious.
- Discoverability balance (client-confirmed sweet spot): the "–" is "present enough
  to remember it exists, quiet enough to ignore" — do NOT make it louder (would
  clutter the 90% narrowing case) nor hidden-on-hover-only (client would forget it
  exists). A one-line sidebar tip reinforces it.

## Gherkin
Feature: Widening and excluding when filtering by label

  Scenario: Widen with "any of these"
    Given labels "recipes" and "dinner" are included
    And the combine mode is "all of these"
    When the client switches to "any of these"
    Then bookmarks with either "recipes" or "dinner" are shown

  Scenario: The all/any switch only appears with 2+ labels
    Given exactly one label is included
    Then no all/any switch is shown
    And the effective mode is narrowing (all)

  Scenario: Exclude a label
    Given "recipes" is included
    When the client clicks the "–" on "dinner"
    Then bookmarks labeled "dinner" are hidden
    And a red "not dinner" chip is shown

## Assumptions
- Include and exclude are mutually exclusive per label (excluding a label removes it
  from the include set and vice versa).
- Exclude and all/any combine with full-text search, which continues to stack.
