Scenario ID: SCN-025
Title: Permanently remove a bookmark with confirmation
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-025
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A bookmark has a permanent removal action. Before anything is deleted, the app explicitly asks for confirmation and explains that the bookmark's personal note and labels are also removed. Confirming deletes the bookmark and everything attached to it; declining leaves it unchanged.

## Gherkin
Feature: Removing bookmarks

  Scenario: Confirm permanent removal
    Given a saved bookmark has a personal note, labels, and Read later status
    When the person chooses to remove the bookmark
    Then the app asks for confirmation before deleting anything
    And explains that its attached information will also be removed
    When the person confirms removal
    Then the bookmark, note, labels, and Read later status are permanently removed

  Scenario: Decline permanent removal
    Given the removal confirmation is visible
    When the person chooses to keep the bookmark
    Then the bookmark and everything attached to it remain unchanged

## Assumptions
- Permanent removal has no time-limited undo banner.
- A reversible way to set bookmarks aside belongs to a later cycle.
