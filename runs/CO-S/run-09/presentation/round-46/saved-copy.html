Designing for Readers

Practical ways to make reading on the web clearer, calmer, and more rewarding.

An article from A List Apart

Reading is one of the web’s most familiar activities, but thoughtful reading experiences rarely happen by accident. Good typography, measured line lengths, and a calm visual rhythm help ideas reach the people they were written for.

A reader needs more than legible letters. Structure provides a map: headings establish hierarchy, paragraphs hold one thought at a time, and useful whitespace lets each idea breathe.

A page designed for reading should make the words feel inevitable, not merely available.

Start with the content’s natural pace. Choose type and spacing that support it, reduce distractions around it, and preserve a clear path from the opening idea to the conclusion.
