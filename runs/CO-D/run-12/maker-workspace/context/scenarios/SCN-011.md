Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: Show a clear empty search result
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When no bookmark matches the entered search words, the search words remain available to correct and the app plainly states that no saved links match. The overall saved-link count remains visible so the user understands that the collection is intact. The app does not guess at alternative searches.

## Gherkin
Feature: Finding bookmarks

  Scenario: No bookmarks match a search
    Given the collection contains saved bookmarks
    When the user types search words that match none of their titles, descriptions, notes, or addresses
    Then no bookmark cards are shown
    And the entered search words remain in the search field
    And the app states that no saved links match
    And the overall saved-link count remains visible
    And the app does not suggest alternative searches

## Assumptions
- A no-match search does not alter the collection.
- No suggestions are shown for a no-match search.
