Scenario ID: SCN-020
Title: Permanently delete several selected bookmarks with confirmation
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-020
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
After selecting several bookmarks, choosing Delete from the bulk-action bar opens a count-specific confirmation. It explains that every selected bookmark and its associated notes, tags, and saved details will be removed and that the action cannot be undone. Permanent removal occurs only after a deliberate second click that names the exact count. Bulk deletion never bypasses the safeguard approved for deleting one bookmark.

## Gherkin
Feature: Permanently delete a selected batch deliberately

  Scenario: Confirm permanent deletion of several bookmarks
    Given two bookmarks are selected
    When the person chooses Delete from the bulk-action bar
    Then a confirmation states that two bookmarks will be deleted
    And explains that their notes, tags, and saved details will also be removed
    And explains that the deletion cannot be undone
    When the person confirms "Delete 2 permanently"
    Then both selected bookmarks and their associated information are permanently removed

## Assumptions
- Cancelling the bulk confirmation leaves every selected bookmark unchanged.
- The confirmation count always reflects the current selection.
