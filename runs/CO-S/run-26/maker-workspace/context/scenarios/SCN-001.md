Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a link and have its page details filled in automatically
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

From the bookmark app, the client pastes (or picks) a link and saves it. The app
reads the page details on its own and, after a brief moment, shows a saved item
as a card in the "Saved" list. The card shows the page title, the site name, a
short description, and the date it was saved — all filled in automatically,
without the client typing any of them. The newest saved link appears at the top
of the list. When a page has no available description, the card still shows at
least the title and site name.

## Gherkin

Feature: Save a link with auto-filled page details

  Scenario: Save a link whose page details are available
    Given the client is on the bookmark app
    And the "Save a link" field is empty
    When the client enters a link and saves it
    Then the app reads the page details automatically
    And a saved item appears at the top of the "Saved" list
    And the item shows the page title, the site name, a short description, and the save date
    And the client did not have to type any of those details

  Scenario: Save a link whose page has no description
    Given the client is on the bookmark app
    When the client enters a link whose page has no available description and saves it
    Then a saved item appears at the top of the "Saved" list
    And the item shows at least the page title and the site name
    And the item shows the save date

  Scenario: Newest saved link ordering
    Given the client has already saved one or more links
    When the client saves another link
    Then the newly saved item appears at the top of the "Saved" list

## Assumptions

- "Useful page details" means: title, site name, short description, save date.
  (Explicitly confirmed by the client.)
- Reading the page details happens automatically on save (simulated in the
  prototype; the real source is the page itself).
- Newest-first ordering is the default list order. (Confirmed by the client.)
