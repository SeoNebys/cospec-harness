Scenario ID: SCN-032
Title: Keep information-dense bookmarks scannable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
In a large collection, a bookmark with a very long title, description, formatted note, and many labels still appears as a compact card. The card previews two lines of title and description, shows the first two labels plus a count of the remainder, and provides Show all details. Opening it reveals the complete title, description, every label, and the full formatted note without losing information.

## Gherkin
Feature: Browsing a large bookmark collection

  Scenario: Scan a bookmark containing abundant information
    Given the collection contains hundreds of bookmarks
    And one bookmark has a long title, description, formatted note, and eight labels
    When the person browses the collection
    Then that bookmark appears as a compact card
    And its title and description are previewed in two lines each
    And its first two labels and the count of remaining labels are shown

  Scenario: Open all details from a compact card
    Given a bookmark card is showing compact previews
    When the person chooses "Show all details"
    Then the complete title and description are shown
    And all labels are shown
    And the complete formatted personal note is shown

## Assumptions
- Compactness applies to collection cards, not to the full-details view.
- No bookmark information is discarded when its card is shortened for display.
