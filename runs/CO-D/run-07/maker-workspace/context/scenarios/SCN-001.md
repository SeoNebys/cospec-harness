Scenario ID: SCN-001
Title: Save a link with page details filled in automatically (preview then confirm)
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client pastes a link into a single input and presses Save. The app retrieves
the page's own details and shows a pre-filled preview of the bookmark (title,
site name, description, site icon, page thumbnail, the link). If everything looks
right, the client confirms with a single Save press — so a routine save is
paste -> Save -> Save. The confirmed bookmark is added to the top of the
collection and shows the page title, site name, description, site icon, page
thumbnail, the link, and the saved date. The client confirmed the routine save
stays quick and that the auto-filled details, page image, and site icon look
right. Correcting details in the preview before confirming is covered by SCN-002.

    Then the app retrieves the page's own details
    And a pre-filled preview of the bookmark is shown
    And when I press Save on the preview without changes

    And it shows the page title, site name, description, site icon, a page
        thumbnail, the link, and the saved date

## Assumptions
- Title, description, site name, site icon, and thumbnail are sourced from the
  actual linked page (not placeholders).
- The preview is pre-filled and immediately confirmable; no retyping is needed
  for a correct save.
- The newest saved bookmark appears at the top of the collection.
