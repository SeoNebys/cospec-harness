Why old recipes still matter

By Mara Bell · 8 minute read

A recipe is more than a list of instructions. It carries the habits, substitutions, and memories of the people who made it.

For many families, handwritten knowledge survives through repetition, even after the original notebook has become fragile.

Preserving those details means keeping not only ingredients, but the context that made them useful.
