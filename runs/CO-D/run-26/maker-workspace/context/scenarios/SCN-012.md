Scenario ID: SCN-012
Title: Archive as a reversible alternative to deletion, in its own view
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Archiving a bookmark takes it out of the everyday collection without deleting it.
Archived bookmarks are completely separate from "All bookmarks" and "Read later",
including their searches — an archived bookmark never appears in those views or
their search/tag results. Archived bookmarks live in their own quieter "Archived"
tab (with a count), where search, tag browsing, and sorting still work within the
archive. Restoring an archived bookmark returns it to the everyday collection
exactly as it was. Archive is reversible; delete is permanent — the two are clearly
distinct.

## Gherkin
Feature: Archive

  Scenario: Archive removes from everyday views
    Given a bookmark in "All bookmarks"
    When the client archives it
    Then it no longer appears in "All bookmarks" or "Read later"
    And it does not appear in searches or tag browsing in those views

  Scenario: Archived items have their own working view
    When the client opens the "Archived" tab
    Then only archived bookmarks are shown with a count
    And search, tag browsing, and sorting work within the archive

  Scenario: Restore returns an item unchanged
    Given an archived bookmark
    When the client restores it
    Then it returns to the everyday collection exactly as it was

  Scenario: Delete still available and permanent
    Given an archived bookmark
    When the client deletes it
    Then it is removed permanently (distinct from archive)

## Assumptions
- Archived is presented as a third, quieter tab (client-confirmed).
- Restore preserves all fields, tags, note, read-later status, and date added.
- Archived state does not affect read-later membership shown in normal views
  (archived items are simply excluded there).
