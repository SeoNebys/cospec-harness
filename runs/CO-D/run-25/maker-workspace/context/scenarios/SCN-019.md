Scenario ID: SCN-019
Title: Add a bookmark to Read later from its card
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-019
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
An already-saved bookmark can be added to Read later with a direct action on its card, without opening the details editor. The card then shows persistent Read later status, independent of its labels.

## Gherkin
Feature: Read later

  Scenario: Mark a saved bookmark to read later
    Given a saved bookmark is visible in the collection
    And it is not in Read later
    When the person uses the Read later action on its card
    Then the bookmark is added to Read later
    And its card visibly shows that status
    And the bookmark's labels are unchanged

## Assumptions
- Adding and removing Read later status are direct card actions.
- Opening the separate Read later collection is explored separately.
