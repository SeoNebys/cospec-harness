# Business goal

## Cycle 1 — confirmed 2026-09-17 (SESSION-001)

### Problem (client's words)
> Right now my bookmarks become a big, messy pile, so I often can't find
> something when I need it.

### Desired outcome (client's words)
> I want to quickly save a link I've just found, have the app pick up its basic
> details for me, and optionally add tags or a note. Later, I want to search or
> filter the collection easily, including keeping "read later" items separate.
> I'd also like to archive links I want to keep without cluttering the main view.

### Core goal (confirmed)
The real goal is **finding the right saved link again later, with confidence** —
not merely storing links. Saving is the easy part; the payoff is reliable
retrieval and keeping the collection from turning back into clutter.

### Priority (confirmed)
1. **Primary:** good search, tags, and filtering (finding things again)
2. **Secondary:** "read later" as a separate lane
3. Supporting: fast save with auto-filled details; optional tags/note; archive

### Interface form (confirmed)
Primarily a **web app used in a browser**, with a layout that also works
comfortably on a phone (responsive).

### Direction confirmation
Client confirmed: "Yes, that's the right direction. Finding things again is the
main priority; 'read later' is useful, but secondary to good search, tags, and
filtering."
