Scenario ID: SCN-019
Title: Archive several selected bookmarks together
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-019
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
After selecting several bookmarks, choosing Archive from the bulk-action bar archives every selected bookmark together. They leave the active list, the All bookmarks and Archive counts both update, and one confirmation states how many were archived and that they remain safe. Bulk Archive preserves exactly the same reversible meaning as archiving one bookmark.

## Gherkin
Feature: Archive several bookmarks in one action

  Scenario: Archive a selected batch
    Given two bookmarks are selected in All bookmarks
    When the person chooses Archive from the bulk-action bar
    Then both selected bookmarks leave the active list
    And the All bookmarks count decreases by two
    And the Archive count increases by two
    And the app confirms that two bookmarks were archived and kept safe
    And both bookmarks remain recoverable from Archive

## Assumptions
- Bulk Archive preserves all bookmark details, tags, and notes.
- No extra confirmation is required because Archive is reversible.
