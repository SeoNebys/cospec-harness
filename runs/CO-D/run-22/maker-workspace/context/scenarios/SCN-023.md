Scenario ID: SCN-023
Title: Restore an archived bookmark with all information intact
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The Archived view shows tucked-away bookmarks with their titles, labels, personal notes, and other information intact. Restoring a bookmark removes it from Archived, returns it to the main collection, updates both counts, and confirms the move without losing any information.

## Gherkin
Feature: Archiving bookmarks

  Scenario: Restore an archived bookmark
    Given an archived bookmark has labels and a personal note
    When the person opens the Archived view
    Then the bookmark appears with its labels and note intact
    When the person restores it to the collection
    Then it no longer appears in Archived
    And it appears in the main collection
    And its labels, note, and other information remain unchanged
    And the All bookmarks and Archived counts are updated
    And the restoration is confirmed

## Assumptions
- Archived bookmarks can be browsed in a dedicated Archived view.
- Restoring returns a bookmark to its prior active state.
