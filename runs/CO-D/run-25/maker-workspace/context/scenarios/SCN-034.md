Scenario ID: SCN-034
Title: Keep long personal notes skimmable
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-034
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When a personal note is long enough to make its bookmark card dominate the list, the card initially shows a compact preview. The person can reveal the complete formatted note with Show full note and collapse it again with Show less. No content or formatting is lost.

## Gherkin
Feature: Personal bookmark notes

  Scenario: Expand and collapse a long note
    Given a bookmark has a long formatted personal note
    When its card appears in the collection
    Then a compact preview of the note is shown
    And the card remains easy to skim alongside other cards
    When the person chooses Show full note
    Then the complete note and its formatting are visible on the card
    When the person chooses Show less
    Then the compact preview is restored

## Assumptions
- Short notes remain fully visible without an expansion control.
