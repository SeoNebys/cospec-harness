Scenario ID: SCN-013
Title: Keep a large collection easy to scan
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-013
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
With hundreds of bookmarks, collection cards remain a consistent, compact size. Unusually long titles and descriptions are shortened on cards so rows remain easy to scan, while full titles, descriptions, and notes remain available on bookmark details views. Search, collection and status counts, and tag filters remain within easy reach while scrolling through the collection.

## Gherkin
Feature: Scan a large bookmark collection

  Scenario: Browse hundreds of bookmarks including unusually long content
    Given the collection contains hundreds of bookmarks
    And a bookmark has an unusually long title or description
    When the user browses the collection
    Then bookmark cards remain consistently sized
    And long title and description previews are shortened on the card
    But the complete title, description, and notes remain on the bookmark details view
    And search, status counts, and tag filters remain readily accessible while browsing

## Assumptions
- The collection uses scrolling rather than requiring the client to manage pages manually.
- Shortening applies only to collection previews and never alters saved content.
