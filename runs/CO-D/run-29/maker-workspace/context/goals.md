# Goal statement — Cycle 1

## Client's words

> Right now saving a link is honestly a bit of a mess for me. What I want is: I paste in a web address and the app just figures out the rest — it grabs the page's title, a short description, the little site icon, maybe a preview picture — so I'm not typing all that myself. And if I want to fix the title or description it pulled, I can, either right then or later.
>
> For finding things, today I basically scroll and squint. I'd want a clean list I can actually read — title, a line of description, the tags, the icon — and clicking one just takes me to the actual page.
>
> Two things that really matter to me: I want to put tags on my bookmarks and then tap a tag to see just those, and I want a proper search that looks through everything — titles, descriptions, my own notes, the address — without me worrying about capital letters. And if I try to save something I already saved, don't make a second copy — just take me to the one I've already got so I can tweak it.
>
> That's the heart of what would feel different.

## Confirmed direction

> Yes, that's the right overall goal — you've captured it well.
>
> The only thing I'd add so we're on the same page: when I search, I don't just want to type a word. I'd like to be able to search by a tag directly, and to combine things — like "show me anything about Rome that's tagged article or book." And searching an exact phrase in quotes should match exactly. But that's detail we can get into later; the big picture you described is exactly what I'm after.

## Confirmed interface form

> A web app, please. I'd like to just open it in my browser on my computer and use it there. It's really just for me — one person, my own collection — so nothing fancy with accounts or logins for other people. Something I can reach from a browser is exactly what I have in mind.
