Title: Remove links — reversible Archive and permanent Delete
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A bookmark can be archived (set aside, fully reversible) or deleted (permanent).
Archive and Delete live in a per-item "⋯" (more) menu to keep cards clean; the
reading action and Edit stay visible. Archived items appear only under a new
"Archived" filter (with a count) and are excluded from All / To read / Read and
from searches in those views, but remain searchable within the Archived view.
In the Archived view, Restore is a visible action (bringing the bookmark back
unchanged) and Delete is in that item's ⋯ menu. Deleting asks for confirmation,
states it cannot be undone, and suggests archiving instead.

## Gherkin
Feature: Remove and restore links

  Scenario: Archive a link (reversible)
    Given an active bookmark
    When the client chooses Archive from its ⋯ menu
    Then it leaves the main lists and searches and appears under the Archived filter
    And the Archived count increases

  Scenario: Restore an archived link
    Given a bookmark in the Archived view
    When the client presses Restore
    Then it returns to the bookmarks unchanged

  Scenario: Delete a link (permanent, confirmed)
    Given a bookmark
    When the client chooses Delete
    Then a confirmation appears warning it cannot be undone and suggesting archive
    When the client confirms
    Then the bookmark is permanently removed
    When the client cancels instead
    Then nothing is removed

  Scenario: Archived items stay out of normal views
    Given an archived bookmark
    Then it does not appear in All, To read, Read, or their searches
    But it is searchable within the Archived view

## Assumptions
- Archiving takes an item out of the reading queue views but preserves its state.
