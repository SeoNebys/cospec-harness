Scenario ID: SCN-013
Title: Save an unreachable page and complete it automatically later
Type: Error Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-013
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
If a submitted page cannot be reached, the client can still save its address with a manually supplied title and notes. The bookmark clearly remains in a waiting state and honestly states that no saved copy exists yet. Keepwell retries automatically without requiring the client to babysit it. When the page becomes reachable, the site icon, description, and readable saved copy are filled in automatically. Any field the client deliberately edited, especially the title, is preserved rather than overwritten by recovered page details.

## Gherkin
Feature: Recover incomplete bookmark captures

  Scenario: Save an unreachable page in a waiting state
    Given a submitted page cannot be reached
    When the client supplies a title and saves it anyway
    Then the address and client-supplied details are saved as a bookmark
    And the bookmark clearly states that page details and a saved copy are still pending
    And automatic capture retries remain scheduled

  Scenario: Complete a bookmark after its page recovers
    Given an incomplete bookmark is waiting for automatic capture
    And the client deliberately edited its title
    When a later retry reaches the page
    Then the description and site icon are filled in
    And a readable saved copy becomes ready
    But the client-edited title remains unchanged

## Assumptions
- Capture retries continue automatically until successful rather than silently giving up.
- A waiting bookmark remains visibly distinguishable from a fully captured bookmark.
- Recovered details fill missing values but never overwrite fields deliberately edited by the client.
