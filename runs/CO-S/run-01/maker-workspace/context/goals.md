# Business goal

## Cycle / status
- Established: Cycle 1, Phase 1 (Goal exploration)
- Interface form: Web app, used in the browser on a computer

## Goal statement (in the client's language)

"I want to stop losing track of stuff I save. Right now I bookmark things in my
browser and never find them again — it's just a giant messy pile. So mainly I
want to save links and actually be able to find them later and keep them a bit
organized."

## What success means

Saving a link is the easy part and is NOT the point. The app succeeds only if
the client can **find a saved link again later**, and stays organized enough
that finding remains easy as the collection grows.

Failure condition (client's words):
"if I save something and can't dig it back out later, it's useless."

## How the client pictures "finding" again
- A mix of two ways, both wanted:
  1. Typing in what they remember and searching for it
  2. Browsing by some kind of category or label

## Verbatim client utterances
- "I want to stop losing track of stuff I save."
- "Right now I bookmark things in my browser and never find them again — it's
   just a giant messy pile."
- "if I save something and can't dig it back out later, it's useless."
- "sometimes I'd just type in what I remember and search for it, and sometimes
   I'd want to browse by some kind of category or label. Both, I think."
- "I picture this in my web browser, on my computer."
