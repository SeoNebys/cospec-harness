Scenario ID: SCN-013
Title: Keep cards scannable when bookmark details are unusually long
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A bookmark with an unusually long title and description remains a tidy, scannable card: each text block is limited to a few lines with an ellipsis. When many labels are assigned, the card shows the first three and a count of the remaining labels rather than growing into a crowded block. Nothing is discarded; the full title, full description, and every label remain available in bookmark details.

## Gherkin
Feature: Bookmark card content boundaries

  Scenario: Display unusually long text on a card
    Given a bookmark has a title and description longer than the card can show neatly
    When the bookmark appears in the library
    Then the title and description are limited to a few lines with an ellipsis
    And the card remains aligned for scanning with other cards
    And the full text remains available in bookmark details

  Scenario: Display many labels on a card
    Given a bookmark has more labels than fit neatly on its card
    When the bookmark appears in the library
    Then the card shows the first three labels
    And shows how many additional labels exist
    And every label remains available in bookmark details

## Assumptions
- Library cards prioritize recognition and scanning over displaying every stored detail.
