Scenario ID: SCN-004
Title: Long title/link is trimmed to a single line
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
When a link's title or address is longer than fits on one line, it is truncated
to a single line ending in "…" rather than wrapping across multiple lines. The
client prioritised quick scanning of the list over seeing the full text inline.

## Gherkin
Feature: Readable list with long content

  Scenario: Very long title is truncated
    Given a saved link whose title is longer than one line
    When the list is displayed
    Then the title is shown on a single line ending with an ellipsis

## Assumptions
- Truncation applies to both the title and the site/address line.
- The full title/address is still available on the underlying link (not lost),
  just not shown in full in the list row.
