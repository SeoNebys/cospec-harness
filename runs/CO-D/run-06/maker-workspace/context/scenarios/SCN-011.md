Scenario ID: SCN-011
Title: "To read" pile — saving auto-adds; opening is only a peek; the client marks read
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
Bookmarks the client hasn't gotten to are kept distinct from ones already dealt with,
addressing the original pain ("tabs left open forever hoping I'll get to them").

- Saving auto-adds a bookmark to the "To read" pile (it starts unread). No manual
  flagging — saving already means "I'll get to this". Zero upkeep.
- Opening/visiting a bookmark is only a peek; it does NOT mark it read. (Client opens
  things, gets pulled away, means to return — a click must not silently clear it.)
- The client marks something read by hand ("Mark read") when actually finished; it
  then leaves the To-read pile. "Mark to-read" puts something back on the pile.
- Unread bookmarks are shown distinct from read ones: an orange left edge, a dot, a
  bolder title, and a small "to read" tag. A "To read" tab/view corrals just the
  unread pile so it doesn't drown among already-handled bookmarks.
- The visual weight is confirmed "clear at a glance, not shouty".

## Gherkin
Feature: Tracking what I haven't read yet

  Scenario: A new save lands on the To-read pile
    When the client saves a new bookmark
    Then it is marked "to read" automatically
    And the To-read count increases

  Scenario: Opening does not mark as read
    Given an unread bookmark
    When the client opens (visits) it
    Then it remains marked "to read"

  Scenario: The client marks something read
    Given an unread bookmark
    When the client chooses "Mark read"
    Then it is no longer on the To-read pile

  Scenario: Put something back on the pile
    Given a read bookmark
    When the client chooses "Mark to-read"
    Then it is on the To-read pile again

## Assumptions
- "To read" (unread) is independent of "set aside" — set-aside items are out of the
  everyday flow entirely; to-read is about not-yet-handled items still in the flow.
- Read/unread is per bookmark; default for a brand-new save is unread.
