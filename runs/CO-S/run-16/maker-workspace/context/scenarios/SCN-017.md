Scenario ID: SCN-017
Title: Expand an unusually content-heavy bookmark in place
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-017
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When a bookmark has an unusually long title, description, personal note, or many tags, its card remains compact by default so the collection stays tidy and easy to scan. A Show more action expands that card in place to reveal all of its context, and Show less returns it to the compact state without leaving the list.

## Gherkin

Feature: Keep content-heavy bookmark cards scannable

  Scenario: Expand and collapse a bookmark with unusually long content
    Given a bookmark has a long title, long description, long personal note, and several tags
    When the bookmark appears in the collection
    Then its card shows a compact preview of that content
    And the card offers a Show more action
    When the user chooses Show more
    Then the full title, description, note, and tags are revealed in place
    And the action changes to Show less
    When the user chooses Show less
    Then the card returns to its compact state

## Assumptions

- Compacting affects presentation only; all content remains saved and searchable.
- Expansion applies to one card at a time and does not navigate away from the collection.
