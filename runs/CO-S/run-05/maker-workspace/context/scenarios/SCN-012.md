Scenario ID: SCN-012
Title: Archiving removes a bookmark from the reading pile
Type: Edge Case
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-012
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour

When the client archives a bookmark from the Read later view, it disappears
from both the reading pile and the active bookmark collection. It remains in
the Archive view with its information intact.

## Gherkin

Feature: Archiving bookmarks

  Scenario: Archive a bookmark that was marked for later
    Given a bookmark is active and marked for later
    When the client archives it from the Read later view
    Then it is not shown in the Read later view
    And it is not shown in the active bookmark collection
    And it is shown in the Archive view with its information intact

## Assumptions

- Archived bookmarks cannot simultaneously remain in the reading pile.
