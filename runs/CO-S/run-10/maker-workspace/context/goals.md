# Business goal

## Cycle 1 — confirmed

### Goal statement (in the client's language)

Reliably re-find a saved link when it's needed. Today links are scattered across
the browser, notes, and messages, so even things saved on purpose get lost — the
client recently knew they'd saved an article for later but couldn't remember its
title or where they'd put it.

The client wants one place where they can:
- quickly save a link
- have its basic details filled in automatically
- organise it with tags and notes
- find it again by searching
- mark things to read later, then archive or delete them when done

### Primacy (confirmed with client)

- **Primary pain:** re-finding links that were already saved (retrieval).
- **Supporting need:** saving must be quick enough that the client consistently
  uses the app instead of scattering links elsewhere.

All other capabilities (auto-filled details, tags, notes, read-later,
archive/delete) are the *means* that make reliable re-finding possible.

### Interface form (confirmed with client)

Web app, opened in a browser, usable on both computer and phone (responsive
layout). Prototyping method: HTML-based interactive prototype.

### Verbatim client utterances

- "I've accumulated lots of bookmarks across my browser, notes, and messages,
  and I often can't find the right link when I need it. Recently I knew I'd saved
  an article for later, but couldn't remember its title or where I'd put it."
- "I want one place where I can quickly save a link, have its basic details
  filled in automatically, organise it with tags and notes, and find it again by
  searching. I'd also like to mark things to read later, then archive or delete
  them when I'm done."
- "Re-finding saved links is the bigger pain, but saving should still be quick
  enough that I'll consistently use the app instead of scattering links
  elsewhere."
