# Non-functional backlog

- Keep the collection visually clean, readable, and quick to scan even with many bookmarks.
- Prefer quiet, compact controls that remain discoverable without crowding every card.
- Use plain, reassuring, non-scolding language for errors and empty states.
- Preserve trust: never make saved information appear lost or silently changed.
- Give immediate, visible feedback when filters, searches, status changes, or retries take effect.
