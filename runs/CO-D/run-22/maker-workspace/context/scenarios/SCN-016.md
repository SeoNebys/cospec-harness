Scenario ID: SCN-016
Title: Mark a read-later bookmark as finished
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
A person can mark an article in Read later as read. It leaves the Read later pile, moves to a separate Finished view, remains in the overall bookmark collection, and updates each count. A brief Undo action provides a safety net for accidental completion.

## Gherkin
Feature: Managing a reading queue

  Scenario: Finish a read-later article
    Given an article is in Read later
    When the person marks it as read
    Then it no longer appears in Read later
    And it appears in Finished
    And it remains in All bookmarks
    And the Read later and Finished counts are updated
    And an Undo action is offered

  Scenario: Undo marking an article as read
    Given an article was just moved from Read later to Finished
    When the person chooses Undo
    Then the article returns to Read later
    And it no longer appears in Finished
    And the counts return to their prior values

## Assumptions
- Read later and Finished are separate views of bookmarks, not deletion states.
- Undo is available briefly immediately after the action.
