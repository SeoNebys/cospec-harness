Scenario ID: SCN-015
Title: Archive and restore a bookmark
Type: Happy Path
Approval status: approved
Approval session: SESSION-001
Approval cycle: 1

## Approved behaviour
The client can archive a bookmark. Archived bookmarks are hidden from every
normal view (All, To read, Finished) and from ordinary searches, and are not
counted there. They appear only in a dedicated Archived view, where they remain
searchable so the client can find one to restore. Restoring a bookmark returns
it to the normal lists with all its details and its previous read/finished
status intact. Archiving is reversible; deletion (SCN-014) is separate and
permanent.

## Gherkin
Feature: Archive bookmarks

  Scenario: Archive hides from normal views and searches
    Given a saved bookmark
    When the client archives it
    Then it no longer appears in the All, To read, or Finished views
    And it does not appear in ordinary searches within those views
    And it appears in the Archived view

  Scenario: Archived items searchable within Archived view
    Given archived bookmarks exist
    When the client searches while in the Archived view
    Then matching archived bookmarks are shown

  Scenario: Restore keeps details and previous status
    Given an archived bookmark that was "Finished" (or "To read") before archiving
    When the client restores it
    Then it returns to the normal lists with all details
    And its read/finished status is the same as before archiving

## Assumptions
- Archived state is independent of read/finished status and is preserved across
  archive/restore.
